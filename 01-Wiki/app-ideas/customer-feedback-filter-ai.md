# Customer Feedback Filter AI

**Summary:** A one-line app idea: an AI that reads through everything customers ask you to change or add in your app, groups it, and hands back the most common requests so you know what actually needs fixing. It turns scattered feedback into a ranked list.

## Key Points
- Takes in all customer change requests and feature asks.
- Finds the ones that come up most often.
- Returns a short list of what to fix, ranked by how many people asked.

## The Idea
As written: an AI that filters through the things customers want implemented or changed in your app, then gives you the most common ones so you know what you have to fix.

## Why It Fits Alongside the Other Products
Both the [[pos-app-feature-list|POS app]] and the [[staff-task-system-blueprint|staff task system]] are aimed at small businesses who will send feedback in scattered form — texts, calls, in-person comments. This would be the layer that turns that noise into a build queue.
