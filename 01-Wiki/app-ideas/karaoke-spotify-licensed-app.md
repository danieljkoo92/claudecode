# Karaoke App with Spotify Licensing

**Summary:** Early-stage idea for a karaoke application built on top of Spotify's music catalog and operated with proper licensing. The core insight is that Spotify has the largest legal music library available — pairing it with a karaoke experience solves the catalog and licensing problems that hold back most karaoke apps.

## Key Points
- Built with or integrated into Spotify's platform.
- Licensing is a deliberate, noted feature — not an afterthought.
- Very early concept; needs significant development to define scope.

## The Problem It Solves
Most karaoke apps have limited song libraries or rely on unlicensed tracks, creating legal risk and poor selection. A Spotify-backed karaoke app would have a massive legal catalog from day one.

## Open Questions to Develop
- Does this use Spotify's existing instrumental tracks or require separate karaoke-specific licenses?
- Standalone app or Spotify integration / mini-app within the Spotify ecosystem?
- Home use vs. commercial venue use (bars, karaoke venues)?
- How does lyrics sync work — partner with a lyrics API like Musixmatch?
- What's the revenue model — subscription, per-use, venue licensing?
