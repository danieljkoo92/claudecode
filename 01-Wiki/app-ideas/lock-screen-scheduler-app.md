# Lock Screen Scheduler App

**Summary:** An app idea for scheduling what your phone's lock screen shows at different times of day. A morning checklist appears from 7:00 to 7:10am, motivational posts after that, a picture of your kid before work, something else at lunch. The reasoning behind it is honest: to-do lists only work if you remember to open them, and a lock screen is the one thing you cannot avoid looking at.

## Key Points
- Schedule the lock screen to change at set times of day.
- Morning slot (7:00–7:10am) shows a **checklist you have to do quickly**.
- After 7:10am it switches to the normal lock screen or motivational posts.
- Before work it becomes something motivating — a picture of your daughter, your family, your mom.
- The whole point: **if you forget to open the to-do list, the to-do list doesn't happen.**

## The Problem It Solves
As written in the note: "I have real trouble going to a to-do list. If I forget to go to the to-do list, I'm not going to do the to-do list." A lock screen gets seen dozens of times a day whether you intend to or not, so putting the list there removes the step where you have to remember.

## Example Day
| Time | Lock screen shows |
|---|---|
| 7:00–7:10am | Morning checklist — quick items to knock out |
| 7:10am | Regular lock screen, or motivational posts to start the day |
| Before work | Picture of your daughter / family / mom — whatever motivates you to work |
| Lunch | Something else, to be decided |

## Extra Idea
Turn the sound off so a scheduled video plays like a silent motivational clip.
