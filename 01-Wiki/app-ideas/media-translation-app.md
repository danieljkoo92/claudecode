# Media Translation App

**Summary:** A one-line app idea: take whatever is about to be output into media and automatically translate it, producing dubs or subtitles. The aim is to remove the manual translation and dubbing step from publishing content.

## Key Points
- Takes content on its way into media form.
- Automatically translates it.
- Outputs either dubbed audio or subtitles.

## The Idea
As written in the source: an app that takes the code or whatever it is about to turn into media and translates it — dubs or subtitles.
