# Media Auto-Translation and Dubbing App

**Summary:** App concept that takes any video or audio content and automatically produces dubbed audio or subtitles in another language. The goal is to remove the expensive manual process of localization so individual creators can reach international audiences without a production budget.

## Key Points
- Input: any video or audio media.
- Output: same content with AI-generated dubbing or subtitles in the target language.
- Removes the manual translation, voice recording, and sync work from the localization pipeline.

## The Problem It Solves
Creators who want to reach global audiences currently need to hire translators and voice actors, then manually sync audio. This app would automate all of that with AI translation and voice synthesis — making international distribution accessible to solo creators.

## Core Features
- Upload or link to any video or audio.
- Select target language(s).
- Generate: subtitles, dubbed audio track, or both.
- Export a publish-ready localized version.
