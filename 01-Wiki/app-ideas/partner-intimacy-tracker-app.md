# Partner Intimacy Tracker App

**Summary:** App concept for logging and replaying what works best with a romantic partner during intimate moments. User inputs the sequence of activities, duration of each, and other details. The app plays music in the background and delivers audio cues and timer reminders during the session — keeping it immersive rather than visual.

## Key Points
- User inputs: sequence of activities, time lengths, any custom notes.
- App plays background music synchronized to the session.
- Delivers audio cues and timer sounds (not visual) during use.
- Builds a history over time so the user can review and improve.

## Core Features
- Input form: sequence + duration + custom parameters.
- Music playback running throughout the session.
- Non-visual audio reminders and countdowns.
- Session log and history for review and refinement.

## Potential Positioning
- Could be marketed as a "relationship wellness" or "intimacy improvement" app for broader appeal.
- Adult platform distribution or private distribution model.
