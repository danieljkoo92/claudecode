# Partner Timing Tracker App

**Summary:** An app idea for privately recording what works with a partner in bed — the order of things and how long each takes. The app then plays music during use and gives small audio cues as reminders and timers, so the user is not looking at a screen.

## Key Points
- User inputs the order, the length of time, and other details.
- Plays music while in use.
- Gives small sounds as reminders and timers rather than visual prompts.

## The Idea
The note describes an app that lets you keep a record of the fastest way to get your partner to orgasm. You input the sequence and the timing, and during use the app plays music while giving small sounds for reminders and timers.
