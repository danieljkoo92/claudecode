# Universal Voice Command System

**Summary:** An idea for a voice command layer built for high-pressure environments, where the operator's hands are busy and there is no time to look at a screen. The same system routes spoken instructions to whatever platform is needed — a POS, a trading platform, hospital prep, EMS dispatch, or email — with a customizable list of items per industry.

## Key Points
- One voice layer, many destinations: **POS, trading platform, hospital prep, EMS dispatch, email**.
- Each industry loads its own **customizable list** of recognised items.
- Restaurants: food and drinks. Stock market: tickers. EMS: what the hospital should prepare.
- The point is hands-free operation when stopping to type is not an option.

## The Idea
A universal voice command system for high-pressure environments that can route instructions to whatever system is needed.

Add any customizable list of things:
- **For restaurants** — food and drinks
- **For the stock market** — stocks, etc.
- **For EMS** — things the hospital should get ready for an incoming emergency visit, so staff are not gathering equipment on the fly when the patient arrives

## Why the EMS Case Is the Strongest
The restaurant version already exists as a feature inside the [[pos-app-feature-list|POS app]] — say "order flow" and it starts listening. The EMS version is the one nobody has: a paramedic reading out what is coming in, so the hospital has the right equipment staged before arrival instead of scrambling at the door.
