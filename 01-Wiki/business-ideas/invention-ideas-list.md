# Invention Ideas List

**Summary:** A running list of quick product and invention ideas, most written as a single line each. They range from physical products like weighted pants and a laundry basket that becomes a chair, to software like a parking-spot tracker and a book translator for difficult text.

## Key Points
- Fifteen ideas, all early-stage one-liners.
- Several are physical products; several are apps.
- Two appear more than once in the source, which usually means they kept coming back: **parking spot tracker** and **inventory app**.
- A few lines are typo-heavy and their meaning is unclear — noted below rather than guessed at.

## Physical Products
- **Laundry basket that turns into a chair**
- **Waterproof socks**
- **Weighted pants**
- **Adjustable screen, any size**
- **Dried ACV and baking soda tablets** for cleaning fruit
- **Mesh net strainer** and other bar tools *(also listed with café equipment)*

## Apps and Software
- **App that pins where you left your car** — records the last time you left it so you know where you parked *(written down twice)*
- **Inventory app** *(written down twice)*
- **Website or app that translates books with hard words** — makes difficult text readable
- **Video alarm**
- **Computer-generated random famous person voice** — with the angle being no lawsuit exposure

## Services
- **Company that stops shoplifters**

## Health and Body
- **A stance that fixes your lower back** — described as a wide lean-back position

## More Ideas From Later in the Notes
- **Hook up Amish workers with construction jobs** — a labour matching service
- **AI camera for shoplifting that talks** — described as "talks hood," so the deterrent is the camera calling people out in a voice that lands rather than a generic alarm
- **Stick with retractable barbs** to instantly stitch any hole
- **Create a limitless pill**
- **Magnet you throw at the wall when you're mad**, with a string to pull it back
- **FedEx desk at the airport** for items that would otherwise be confiscated — mail it to yourself instead of binning it
- **Air holes for a funnel**

The FedEx-at-the-airport one is the most immediately sellable of these: the problem is universal, the cost of testing it is a table and a contract, and every traveller who has had something taken at security already understands the product without explanation.
