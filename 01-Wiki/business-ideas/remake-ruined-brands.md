# Remake Ruined Brands

**Summary:** A business idea for a company that remakes products people loved before a big corporation bought them and changed them. The note names Cholula, Naked Juice and donuts as examples of things that got worse after acquisition.

## Key Points
- Target: products that were loved, then ruined by big companies.
- Named examples: **Cholula**, **Naked Juice**, **donuts**.
- The pitch is bringing back the original quality, not inventing something new.

## The Idea
As written: a company that remakes everything that was loved and then ruined by big companies — Cholula, Naked Juice, donuts, etc.

## Why It Could Work
When a large company buys a small brand, the recipe usually gets cheaper and regulars notice immediately. That leaves a group of customers who already know the product and are unhappy with what it became — an audience that does not need to be taught what the thing is.
