# Streamer and Celebrity Gift Delivery Service

**Summary:** Business idea for a company that receives fan gifts on behalf of streamers and celebrities through a PO box, then delivers them discreetly so the public figure never has to share their real address. Solves a real safety and logistics problem that prevents many creators from accepting fan gifts at all.

## Key Points
- Acts as a trusted middleman between fans and public figures.
- Receives all gifts at a PO box on the public figure's behalf.
- Delivers to the real recipient discreetly — their address stays private.
- Target market: Twitch streamers, YouTubers, influencers, celebrities.

## The Problem It Solves
Fans regularly want to send physical gifts to creators they follow. Most creators cannot accept gifts safely because receiving packages requires sharing a real address — a serious privacy and safety risk for anyone with a public following. This business creates a secure, anonymous pipeline for that gifting.

## Potential Business Model
- Charge senders a per-package handling and forwarding fee.
- Charge creators/celebrities a monthly PO box management subscription.
- Premium tiers: gift wrapping, unboxing video documentation, white-glove delivery.
