# Streamer and Celebrity Gift Delivery Service

**Summary:** A business idea for a company that accepts gifts meant for streamers and celebrities and delivers them discreetly from a PO box. It solves the problem that public figures cannot receive fan mail without giving out a real address.

## Key Points
- Company accepts gifts on behalf of streamers and celebrities.
- Gifts are delivered from a PO box, discreetly.
- The recipient's real address never gets exposed.

## The Idea
As written: a company that accepts gifts for streamers and celebrities and delivers them from a PO box discreetly.

## Why It Could Work
Fans want to send things to creators they follow, and creators mostly cannot accept them because taking delivery means giving out an address. A middleman that receives, screens and forwards packages removes that problem for both sides.
