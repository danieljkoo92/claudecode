# Pickup Lines

**Summary:** Four one-liners written down together — flirt lines rather than stage material, though a couple could work either way. Short enough that they are either used as-is or dropped into a bit about trying them.

## Key Points
- Four lines, all short.
- The "rent" one is the strongest — it has an actual turn in it.
- The last one is the most aggressive and would land better on stage than in person.

## The Lines
- "You're pretty, I'm cute — we would be pretty cute."
- "I think you owe me rent, you been in my mind and some shit."
- "I got a problem, can you help me? I think you're cute and I don't have your number."
- "You got a boyfriend? Why don't you trade him in for a man."

## Also In the Notes
A related bit filed elsewhere: trading an umbrella for a girl's number — *"if I'd walk in the rain for someone who might be my girlfriend, imagine what I'd do if she actually was."* That one has a real structure to it and could be built out.
