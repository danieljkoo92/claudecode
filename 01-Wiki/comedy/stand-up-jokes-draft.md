# Stand-Up Jokes — Draft Material

**Summary:** A collection of draft stand-up comedy jokes and observational bits. Topics include race and crime statistics, straight vs. gay social dynamics, unemployment humor, John Crist, Luis CK's take on intent in crime, personal childhood stories, Golden Ticket / Make-A-Wish, Latino-Asian cultural crossover, elderly father surgery, and bodily humor. Material is raw and unfiltered — these are working notes, not a polished set.

## Key Points
- Mix of observational, self-deprecating, and taboo-topic comedy.

- Some bits play on crime statistics and racial stereotypes in a comedic frame.
