# Stand-Up Material — Batch 2

**Summary:** A second, larger set of stand-up premises found in the notes file, separate from the filed joke sheet. It includes longer worked-out bits — the MMA cage story, the trans/pronoun argument, the illegal immigration bit — alongside a lot of one-liners. Some material overlaps with the first batch; only the premises that are genuinely new are recorded here.

## Key Points
- Contains the **longest worked-out bits** in the material — the pronoun argument and the MMA story both have real structure.
- Heavy on race, body, and culture-war material, same as batch 1.
- Several one-liners are close to finished; most bits still need tightening.
- Overlapping material with [[stand-up-material-draft|batch 1]] has been left out rather than duplicated.

## The Pronoun Bit (most developed)
> Trans people — it's nothing against you. But don't try to get me to call something different than what I've been calling it my whole life without putting in any effort.
>
> I'm going to explain exactly why this is not transphobia. I have close friends who would kill for me and never snitch, do 5–10 years in jail for me. If I asked one of them today to start calling me Brian all of a sudden, they'd laugh in my face, call me a retard, and call me what they've always called me. Notice that has **nothing** to do with gender.
>
> But if I legally changed my name to Brian and had a reason, they wouldn't be so against it. That's the point — you can't put on a pink t-shirt and demand I call you a woman. Again, nothing to do with gender.
>
> If you cut off your legs and arms and put wheels on instead, I'll call you Optimus Prime if you want.

## The MMA Story
> I realized MMA wasn't for me. I got punched in the mouth and wanted to tap and stop the match — but I was still standing. So I realized I had to crouch down to tap the floor. Then I realized he might kick me in the mouth while I was crouching, because he was still right in front of me. So I just jumped the cage.
