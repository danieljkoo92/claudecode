## Growing Up Korean in New York
- Grew up in an Asian hood. But what's a hood without black people. First couple arrests I'd get locked up with my Asian friend for stealing bikes, then my black and Spanish friends for robbing some kid. Oddly enough my Asian friends ended up more criminal than my black and Puerto Rican friends. This is how I talk, if you don't like it you can go fuck yourself.
- This dude said "are you Chinese?" I said no, I'm Korean. He said "what part of China is that?"
- I like to scare black people. Like "your dog is so beautiful, she almost too pretty to eat. Back in my home country we'd name you lunch." Then they get mad — don't talk to my dog like that. Then I say I'm just kidding, I was born here, I've only been to Korea like 14 times.
- Chinatown is the only place in the city with street names instead of numbers. Like white people were like, these Asians are good with numbers, let's fuck with them by naming all the streets after random shit. Canal. Lafayette.
- All South American people call all Asians *chino*, which translates to Chinese. So I've decided to call all South American people Mexican. Fair is fair. But moving on — any New Yorker on the subway has seen a Mexican lady selling candy. Most of them have babies. That's statistically impossible. Some of them MUST be borrowing them babies.
- Latino people, y'all call all Asians chino, that's racist as fuck. But my Latina friend told me y'all don't got a word for it.
- I feel like the Indians that were too dumb to become doctors all became meter maids.
- One of them dark Indians is a Somalian with some curry on him.
