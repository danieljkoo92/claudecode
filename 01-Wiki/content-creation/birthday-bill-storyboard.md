# Birthday Bill Storyboard

**Summary:** An eight-scene storyboard for an animated video that takes a birthday group from party excitement, through the horror of a giant shared bill, to the calm of a private karaoke room with prepaid check-in. The most detailed instruction in it is about faces: characters must not all be smiling, and the relief at the end has to look different from the hype at the start.

## Key Points
- Emotional arc, stated explicitly: **excitement → confusion → tension → chaos → exhaustion → relief**.
- **Do not make every character smile** — expressions must show stress, awkwardness, annoyance, confusion.
- Scene 7's happiness must read as **relief**, visibly different from Scene 1's party energy.
- Ends on the product: private room, drinks already arranged, prepaid check-in.
- Generate each scene separately, reusing the **same reference image** for character consistency.

## The Emotional Map
| Stage | Feeling |
|---|---|
| Beginning | excited, confident, social |
| Middle | stressed, overwhelmed, emotionally drained |
| End | relieved, relaxed, genuinely happy |

## Scene Breakdown

**Scene 1 — "Birthday Vibes"** · Luxury nightclub VIP section. Genuine laughter, excited eyes, energetic body language, friends cheering, birthday girl confident and glowing. Bottle service, sparklers, cocktails, selfies, flashing lights.

**Scene 2 — "The Bill Arrives"** · Confusion starts. Birthday girl's smile fading, raised eyebrows, staring at an oversized itemized receipt, awkward silence, nervous glances, one friend suddenly serious. Music atmosphere dulling. **No big smiles.**

**Scene 3 — "Payment Problems"** · Social discomfort. Defensive friend, confused friend, one pretending to check their phone, guilty expressions, one quietly leaving, birthday girl visibly stressed. Crossed arms, avoided eye contact, fake half-smiles.

**Scene 4 — "Chaos"** · Arguments and confusion.

**Scene 5 — "Birthday Ruined"**

**Scene 6 — "Freeze Transition"**

**Scene 7 — "Private Karaoke Solution"** · Private room, relief and comfort. Authentic relaxed smiles, genuine laughter, calm energy. Singing together, cozy lighting, drinks already arranged, no tension. **The happiness here must feel different from Scene 1 — relief, not hype.**

**Scene 8 — "Prepaid Option"** · Easy and organized. Prepaid confirmations, "PAID" checkmarks, easy check-in, stress-free.

## The Expression Rule
> DO NOT make every character smile. Expressions MUST accurately reflect: stress, awkwardness, tension, annoyance, confusion, emotional exhaustion, relief. Avoid generic "happy party faces." Use believable cinematic acting.

**Avoid:** identical smiles, repetitive expressions, static poses, generic party reactions.
**Prioritize:** expressive eyes, subtle emotional acting, believable body language, cinematic realism.

## What It's Actually Selling
The video sells two things at once: the private room, and **prepaid booking**. The whole middle section is built around the pain of splitting a bill in a group, which is exactly the problem the [[pos-app-feature-list|POS split-bill feature]] solves and the reason the deposit exists on every reservation.
