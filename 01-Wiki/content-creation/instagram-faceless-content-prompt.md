# Instagram Faceless Content Prompt

**Summary:** A ready-to-use AI prompt for generating Instagram content for the karaoke bar, written so every idea works without showing anyone's face. It sets the visual vocabulary — neon, microphones, drinks pouring, silhouettes — and then asks for seven specific deliverables including reel concepts, hooks built from customer frustrations, 30-second scripts, on-screen social proof captions, and comment-driving CTAs.

## Key Points
- Everything must be **faceless** — no talking heads.
- Visual vocabulary is fixed up front: neon lights, karaoke rooms, mics, drinks pouring, song screens, silhouettes.
- Seven deliverables in one prompt, from reel concepts to carousel tips to CTAs.
- Hooks are capped at **10 words**, carousel tips at **12 words**.
- Explicitly bans clichés and generic CTAs like "follow for more".

## The Prompt
```
You are a social media strategist specializing in rapid Instagram growth for
local nightlife and entertainment venues.

Your goal is to generate high-performing faceless Instagram content ideas for a
karaoke bar. The audience is people looking for fun nightlife experiences,
birthdays, group outings, and casual nights out with friends.

All content must be faceless. Use visuals such as:
• neon lights
• karaoke rooms
• microphones
• drinks being poured
• song screens
• people entering rooms (no faces shown)
• close-ups of hands holding microphones
• crowd silhouettes
• drinks clinking
• room lighting and atmosphere

Do not rely on talking heads. Generate content using the structure below.

VIRAL REEL CONCEPTS
Analyze common viral Instagram trends in nightlife, bars, and entertainment.
Create 10 short faceless Reel ideas with high share potential. For each:
• Hook text (max 10 words)
• What the video visually shows (faceless shots)
• Why it will perform well

AUDIENCE PAIN → VIRAL HOOKS
List the 10 biggest frustrations or desires people have when planning a night
out (expensive nightlife, boring bars, not knowing what to do with friends,
birthday planning stress, crowded clubs). Turn each into a Reel hook under 10
words. Avoid clichés. Make them provocative and curiosity-driven.

30-SECOND FACELESS REEL SCRIPTS
Create 5 short scripts, each structured as:
  Irresistible hook
  Short engaging story
  Clear call-to-action
Use short sentences. Include visual instructions for faceless filming.

SOCIAL PROOF TEXT FOR ON-SCREEN CAPTIONS
Generate 10 short on-screen phrases (max 10 words) communicating social proof
or curiosity — packed rooms, birthdays, group parties, late night fun, people
choosing karaoke instead of bars. These work as overlays on faceless videos.

SAVE-WORTHY CAROUSEL CONTENT
Create 5 practical tips for a perfect karaoke night. Each tip 12 words or
fewer. The goal is posts people save.

CONTENT REPURPOSING
Take this concept: "Why karaoke nights are better than regular bars."
Turn it into: a 7-second Reel script, a 5-slide Instagram carousel, and a
single static post with a strong statement.

HIGH-CONVERSION COMMENT CTAs
Create 15 short CTAs designed to trigger comments or saves. Use curiosity or
interaction — commenting a favourite song, birthday planners commenting,
friend groups tagging each other. Avoid generic phrases like "follow for more."

All outputs must be clear, punchy, and optimized for Instagram Reels. Focus on
visual storytelling that works without showing faces.
```

## Why Faceless
It removes the biggest bottleneck in posting consistently — needing someone willing to be on camera. Every shot on the list can be filmed in an empty room before opening.
