# Instagram Prompt Pack

**Summary:** Six general-purpose Instagram growth prompts transcribed from a carousel, each with a one-line explanation of why it works. They cover carousels built for saves, caption rewriting, hook generation, a 30-day strategy, reverse-engineering competitors, and script structure. Unlike the karaoke-specific prompts, these are niche-agnostic — you fill in your own niche.

## Key Points
- Six prompts: carousel generator, caption engine, hook generator, strategy architect, research engine, script builder.
- The organizing principle: **saves signal value, shares expand reach**.
- The hook framework is **Who / What / How** — miss one and retention drops.
- The research prompt is the most valuable: it decodes patterns instead of copying trends.

## 1. Save-Optimized Carousel Generator
> Generate 12 Instagram carousel concepts specifically designed to maximize saves and shares. Each concept must have high education density and clear practical value. Include structured formats such as step-by-step blueprints, framework breakdowns, checklists, common mistakes, myths vs truth comparisons, before-and-after transformations, case studies, and swipe-to-learn series. Each idea should be designed to increase reading time and screenshot intent while positioning me as an authority in my niche.

*Saves signal value. Shares expand reach.*

## 2. Caption Engine
> Rewrite this caption using emotional storytelling and simple, conversational language. Keep readability at grade 6–7 level. Add subtle authority positioning without sounding salesy. Include a save trigger, a comment trigger, and a clear but natural call to action. Integrate friendly keywords relevant to my niche. The caption should feel human, reflective, and psychologically engaging, not robotic or overly structured.

*Captions reinforce emotion. Emotion increases action.*

## 3. Hook Generator (Who / What / How)
> Generate 20 high-retention Instagram hooks for my niche. Every hook must clearly answer three questions within the first line: Who is this for? What is this about? How does this help them? The hook must instantly communicate clarity and value. Use a mix of pattern interrupts, authority positioning, contrarian angles, curiosity gaps, and outcome-driven framing. Avoid vague or clever phrasing. The goal is immediate relevance and psychological pull, not creativity for its own sake.

*Who creates clarity. What builds context. How delivers value. Miss one and retention drops.*

## 4. Content Strategy Architect
> Create a 30-day Instagram content strategy for my niche. Define 4 to 5 core content pillars aligned with audience pain points and desired outcomes. Map the audience journey from awareness to trust, authority, and conversion. Recommend how to sequence topics strategically so each post builds on the previous one. Specify when to educate, when to share stories, when to build authority, and when to introduce offers. Allocate formats intentionally between reels, carousels, and stories. This must feel like a structured growth blueprint, not a random posting calendar.

*Consistency alone doesn't scale. Structured sequencing does.*

## 5. Research Engine (Content Intelligence Layer)
> Act as a senior Instagram content strategist and behavioral analyst. Study the top-performing posts in my niche and reverse engineer why they worked. Analyze at least 10 viral posts and identify recurring hook structures, emotional triggers, positioning angles, psychological drivers behind saves and shares, call-to-action timing, and topic repetition patterns. Explain how attention is captured, how retention is maintained, and what creates conversion. Identify content gaps competitors are missing and suggest strategic opportunities I can leverage to stand out. Present this as applied insights I can directly implement, not surface observations.

*This turns AI into a content intelligence system. You stop copying trends and start decoding patterns.*

## 6. High-Retention Script Builder
> Write a high-retention Instagram script on this topic. Before writing, choose the most psychologically effective persuasion framework for maximum retention. You may use AIDA, PAS, Value-Delayed Framework, Open Loop Technique, Story-Bridge-Offer, Before-After-Bridge, or the 4U Framework. Select the framework intentionally based on the topic and explain the structural flow internally before drafting. The script must increase completion rate, create tension before delivering value, and build curiosity that sustains attention until the final line.

*Structure creates completion. Completion drives distribution.*
