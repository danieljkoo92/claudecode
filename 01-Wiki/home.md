## Patterns Worth Knowing

**Three of your four chargebacks** involved a large discount or a split cash/card payment — the final number never matched what the customer expected to see. Writing the discount and the cash/card split on the itemized receipt would stop most of them being filed at all.

**Both disputes you documented were won on the same two facts:** the itemized receipt was shown before charging, and ID was verified against the card. Keep doing both, every time.

**The most repeated complaint in your own notes** is that nobody tracks where customers come from. The fix already exists in your material — [[how-did-you-find-us-card|the "How did you find us?" card]] — and costs the price of printing.
