STEP 12 — CREATE ENERGY SAVE SCORE

Tell Codex:

“Create the Energy Save Score from 0 to 100.”

Meaning:

0–25:
normal rotation likely

26–50:
slight chance of extended bench minutes

51–75:
starter rest becoming likely

76–90:
early starter rest likely

91–100:
garbage-time bench very likely

Energy Save Score should consider:
• score margin
• quarter
• time remaining
• regular season or playoffs
• playoff round
• series game number
• series score
• elimination game
• closeout game
• starter minutes already played
• rest days before game
• days until next game
• foul trouble
• injuries/minutes restrictions if available
• home or away
• coach history if available

Rules:
• Big lead late increases score.
• Blowout lead late greatly increases score.
• Playoff game lowers score unless lead is very large.
• Game 7 lowers score.
• Elimination game lowers score.
• Team leading series 3-0 or 3-1 increases score when also leading big late.
• Team facing elimination lowers score.
• High starter minutes increase score.
• Short rest before next game increases score.
• Close game keeps score low.
