# Birthday Packages and Themed Rooms

**Summary:** A product and marketing plan for birthday bookings, built around a themed private karaoke night where staff set up two projectors to turn the room into any theme the customer picks. It includes a $40 setup fee, a list of example themes, and three birthday packages at $35, $50 and a longer "here to stay" tier. The page is aimed at birthday searches the venue currently runs no ads for.

## Key Points
- The whole idea starts from a gap: **"birthday ideas" gets searches we don't run ads for.**
- **$40 setup fee** — staff set up and adjust 2 projectors onto opposite walls to theme the room.
- Rooms take **2–40 people**; customers pick **3 themes** in advance and get **2 changes** during the session.
- Birthday packages start at **$35 minimum (4 people minimum)**.
- The birthday person gets a **free birthday shot** — ID checked to verify the date.
- Theme changes must be requested **at least one week in advance**.

## The Page Concept
Header: **BIRTHDAY IDEAS** — chosen because it likely gets a lot of searches the venue is not advertising against.

Themed private karaoke birthday night. Pick your room, up to 2–40 people. For a small setup fee of **$40**, staff set up and adjust two projectors onto both walls parallel to each other, turning the room into any theme of your choice. You get 3 pre-picked themes and can have 2 changes during your session.

Positioned as great for kids, die-hard fans of any genre, or anyone who just wants the vibes.

## Example Themes
- Galaxy
- Frozen
- Disneyland
- Submarine
- Jurassic Park
- Penthouse view
- Castle

Anything else the customer wants, staff will try to provide. **Staff must be informed at least one week in advance of any changes.**

## Birthday Packages
ID is checked to verify the date. The birthday boy / girl / they / them — whichever they choose — gets an extra free birthday shot.

| Package | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum** | $35 | 2 hours karaoke, 1 drink, 1 shot (minimum 4 people) |
| **Pregame** | $50 | 2 hours, 2 house drinks, 2 shots |
| **Here to Stay** | *(price not set)* | 3 hours, 5 drinks, 5 shots |

The "Here to Stay" package has no price written down yet — that needs deciding before this goes live.
