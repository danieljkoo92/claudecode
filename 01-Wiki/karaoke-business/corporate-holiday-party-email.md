# Corporate Holiday Party Email

**Summary:** A formal outreach email pitching Christmas Karaoke as a corporate holiday party venue, aimed at Long Island organizations. It is written in a deliberately businesslike register for someone who has to get the booking approved internally, and it leads on BYO food and drink as a cost-control argument rather than as a party feature.

## Key Points
- Target: **Long Island organizations** — Nassau County and western Suffolk.
- The core selling point is **bring your own food and beverages** = budget control.
- Positions against Manhattan venues on **travel, congestion and parking**.
- Private enclosed rooms framed as "professional and non-crowded".
- Offers to supply an **itemized cost overview suitable for internal approval**.

## The Email
```
Dear [Name],

I am writing to introduce Christmas Karaoke, a private-room venue in Bayside,
Queens, as a potential option for your organization's corporate holiday party or
year-end corporate event.

Our venue provides fully private rooms designed to accommodate corporate groups
in a contained and controlled environment. We are frequently selected by Long
Island-based organizations due to the following considerations:

- Bring-your-own food and beverages, allowing organizations full flexibility in
  catering selection and budget management
- Convenient access from Nassau County and western Suffolk, avoiding the travel
  burden and congestion associated with Manhattan venues
- Ample nearby parking, simplifying logistics for attendees
- Private, enclosed rooms, ensuring a professional and non-crowded setting
  suitable for corporate gatherings

Cost-control justification:
The ability to bring outside food and beverages allows organizations to manage
overall event costs more efficiently compared to venues with mandatory in-house
catering or minimum spend requirements.

If helpful, I would be pleased to provide:

- Room configurations based on anticipated attendance
- December availability options
- A clear and itemized cost overview suitable for internal approval

Please feel free to contact me should you wish to explore this option further or
request additional information.

Kind regards,
Daniel Choi, Assistant Manager
Personal Mobile 917-245-8685
Christmas Karaoke
47-29 Bell Blvd, Bayside, NY 11361
718-224-2434
Info@karaokexmas.com
```

## Why This Email Works
It is written for the person who has to justify the expense to someone else, not for the person who wants to sing. Every bullet answers an objection an approver would raise — cost, travel, parking, professionalism — and the offer of an itemized cost overview hands them the document they need to get sign-off.

It also turns the venue's two supposed weaknesses into advantages: being in Bayside rather than Manhattan becomes a convenience argument, and allowing BYO becomes a budget argument.
