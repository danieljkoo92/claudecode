# Influencer Outreach DM

**Summary:** A short direct message template for inviting influencers to Christmas Karaoke. It opens with a specific compliment about one of their videos, offers a complimentary private karaoke session for them and their friends, and asks for an Instagram post in return. Ends with a low-friction ask — just reply "yes".

## Key Points
- Opens with a **specific** video reference, not generic praise — the blank must be filled in.
- Offers a **complimentary private session** for the influencer and their friends.
- States the expectation plainly: an Instagram post about the experience.
- Closes with the easiest possible reply: **"yes"**.

## The Template
```
Hi! Love your content especially the video with.......

We're CHRISTMAS KARAOKE, a karaoke bar with over 15 private rooms in Bayside
Queens.

We'd love to host you and your friends for a complimentary private karaoke
session! We offer this in the hope that you'll share your experience with your
followers in an Instagram post.

If you're interested, just reply with a "yes" and we can send over the details!
```

## Second Version — The 50% Off Offer
A different, longer version offers a discount rather than a free session, and scales to a much bigger group:

```
Hello my name is Daniel, the new manager here at Christmas Karaoke. I would like
to offer one of our private rooms for 50% off for you and up to 20 additional
guests, given you would post a brief video as an Instagram post showing what a
great time you had in one of our private rooms.

Our rooms have 100,000 American songs and thousands of Spanish songs as well.
You can also bring your own alcohol!

Please feel free to contact the store at 718-224-2434 and ask for me directly,
or any of the employees can also supply further details. Don't forget to mention
we offered 50% off via Instagram to get a quote on the discounted price from
this offer. Hope to hear from you soon!

My personal number is 917-245-8685 — feel free to reach out to me here as well.
Please text first mentioning this message. Thank you.
```

**Which version to use:** the free session works for larger accounts you really want. The 50% offer scales — it works for mid-size accounts, brings up to 21 people through the door, and still collects half the room rate plus whatever they spend. It also mentions the Spanish song library and BYOB, which the shorter DM leaves out.

Note that it asks them to mention the Instagram offer to get the discounted quote — which, like the [[beats-weekly-specials-email|weekly specials email]], makes the campaign trackable.

## Using It Well
- **Always fill in the blank.** A generic "love your content" reads as a mass DM; naming an actual video is the difference between a reply and a scroll past.
- Bringing friends is the point — it fills a room, which produces better footage than one person alone.
- Weeknights are the right slots to offer, since the room would otherwise sit empty. See [[venue-pricing-and-targets|Venue Pricing and Revenue Targets]].
