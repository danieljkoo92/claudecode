# Influencer Outreach DM

**Summary:** A short direct message template for inviting influencers to Christmas Karaoke. It opens with a specific compliment about one of their videos, offers a complimentary private karaoke session for them and their friends, and asks for an Instagram post in return. Ends with a low-friction ask — just reply "yes".

## Key Points
- Opens with a **specific** video reference, not generic praise — the blank must be filled in.
- Offers a **complimentary private session** for the influencer and their friends.
- States the expectation plainly: an Instagram post about the experience.
- Closes with the easiest possible reply: **"yes"**.

## The Template
```
Hi! Love your content especially the video with.......

We're CHRISTMAS KARAOKE, a karaoke bar with over 15 private rooms in Bayside
Queens.

We'd love to host you and your friends for a complimentary private karaoke
session! We offer this in the hope that you'll share your experience with your
followers in an Instagram post.

If you're interested, just reply with a "yes" and we can send over the details!
```

## Using It Well
- **Always fill in the blank.** A generic "love your content" reads as a mass DM; naming an actual video is the difference between a reply and a scroll past.
- Bringing friends is the point — it fills a room, which produces better footage than one person alone.
- Weeknights are the right slots to offer, since the room would otherwise sit empty. See [[venue-pricing-and-targets|Venue Pricing and Revenue Targets]].
