# Large Party Quote — Worked Example

**Summary:** A worked-out quote for a 40-person party, showing exactly how a bill builds from room rate through tax, gratuity and card fee to a $2,257.07 total. It also documents the policy that the card fee is waived if the bill is paid in full in cash.

## Key Points
- 40 people at **$37 each** for the room = $1,480.
- Items ("All in a cup") at **$5.95 each** × 40 = $238.
- Subtotal **$1,718** → total **$2,257.07** after tax, gratuity and card fee.
- **The card fee is removed if the bill is paid in full in cash.**
- Tax, gratuity and card fee add roughly **31%** to the subtotal.

## The Quote
```
Room rate:  37 × 40  = $1,480
Items:      All in a cup 5.95 × 40 = $238
Subtotal:              $1,718

Tax                    $152.52
Gratuity               $326.42
Card fee               $60.13
( We remove the card fee if the bill is paid in full in cash )

TOTAL                  $2,257.07
```

## Checking the Percentages
Against the rates given in [[school-field-trip-quote-email|the school field trip quote]]:
- Tax $152.52 on $1,718 = **8.875%** ✓
- Gratuity $326.42 on $1,718 = **19%** ✓
- Card fee $60.13 on $1,718 = **3.5%** ✓

All three match, so this template can be reused with confidence for any party size.

## The Cash Discount
Waiving the 3.5% card fee for cash saves the customer $60 on a bill this size, and saves the venue the same amount in processing costs — so it costs nothing to offer and gives the customer a reason to say yes. Worth mentioning out loud when presenting a large quote.
