# Venue Pricing and Revenue Targets

**Summary:** The core business facts for Christmas Karaoke, pulled from the briefing text written into several marketing prompts. It covers room pricing, package range and the saving they represent, the birthday champagne offer, opening hours, who the main customers are, and the weekly revenue targets. This is the reference sheet to quote from whenever pricing or positioning comes up.

## Key Points
- **15 private rooms** plus a full bar and a large song library.
- Rooms average **$10 per person per hour**.
- Packages run **$21–$45 per person**, saving customers roughly **$15+ per person**.
- Birthday groups booking packages get a **free bottle of champagne**.
- Short-term revenue goal **$10,000/week**; next goal **$15,000/week**.
- **Friday and Saturday are busy. Sunday through Thursday are slow** — that is the problem to solve.

## The Venue
Christmas Karaoke, Queens, New York. A large private-room karaoke venue with 15 rooms, a full bar, and one of the largest song libraries in the area. Cozy and casual but big enough for large parties.

## Pricing
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Room, per person per hour | ~$10 |
| Packages, per person | $21–$45 |
| Customer saving vs. hourly | ~$15+ per person |
| Birthday groups booking a package | Free bottle of champagne |

## Hours
- **Weekdays:** 4 PM – 4 AM
- **Weekends:** 3 PM – 4 AM

## Customers
Mainly **birthdays** and **friend groups**, plus celebrations generally.

## Revenue Targets
| Goal | Weekly revenue |
|---|---|
| Short term | $10,000 |
| Next | $15,000 |

## The Strategic Position
Stated plainly in the source: **the venue has enough rooms that filling them with groups matters more than maximizing alcohol sales.** That is the same conclusion reached independently in [[marketing-and-byob-strategy|Marketing and BYOB Strategy]], which proposes charging for BYOB once the rooms are full.
