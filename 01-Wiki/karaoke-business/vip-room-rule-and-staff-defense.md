# VIP Room Rule and Staff Defense

**Summary:** A written response to being told he does nothing and could be replaced. It documents keeping short-staffed shifts running, challenges a sales comparison against another location, and records the VIP room upsell rule he introduced — call any party over 17 people and sell them the VIP room, which adds $40–50 an hour and frees up smaller rooms to sell separately.

## Key Points
- **The VIP rule:** any booking over **17 people** gets called and sold the VIP room.
- That adds **$40–50 per hour** per VIP room, and frees rooms 1 and 2 to sell to other parties.
- Before the rule, the VIP room was being given away at **$180** for an 18-person party.
- Sales comparison: the other store does ~**$20k** Saturdays, this one ~**$12k** — but with fewer rooms and shorter hours.
- If open until **4am**, the estimate is **$16–17k** on a Saturday, which is the same volume per room-hour.

## The VIP Room Rule
Two months before the room price change, a staff member was giving the VIP room away at **$180** to an 18-person reservation, because rooms 1 and 2 felt tight for that group.

The rule introduced instead: **anything over 17 people, call them and sell the VIP room.** Two effects:
1. The VIP room sells at its proper rate — **$40–50 more per hour**.
2. Rooms 1 and 2 stay free and can be sold to other parties, creating additional room-hours out of the same night.

There is also a service point attached: when working the tablet, the goal is maximizing rooms **and** calling customers to check they are okay with a smaller room — rather than quietly squeezing them in with no warning.

## The Sales Comparison
On being compared unfavourably to another location:

> It's not like they're doing $20k in sales every Saturday. If they do $20k every Saturday and we do $12k, we're doing the same amount of volume based on the rooms we have and the hours we're open. If we opened until 4am we'd be doing $16–17k on Saturday.

And on whose responsibility walk-in volume is:

> We do the most we can with the people who walk in. It's not like anyone went and brought those customers in from outside. And that's a different borough. **If people don't come into the store, that's entirely on marketing and upper management.**

## The Direct Response
> I don't want to hear that I don't do anything and can just be replaced. I bring extra cash from BYOB. If anyone could do it, why hasn't it been done?

## Worth Extracting
The VIP room rule is the most reusable thing in this note and belongs in the front desk procedure rather than buried in an argument — **over 17 people, call and sell the VIP room.** It is a standing rule that makes money on every large booking.
