# Website Listing Copy

**Summary:** The short marketing copy used to describe the venue online, together with the correct opening hours. It leads on having the largest private karaoke rooms in New York, rooms for up to 50 guests, and a catalogue of over 100,000 songs including thousands of international titles.

## Key Points
- Headline claim: **largest private karaoke rooms in NY**.
- Open **7 days**.
- **Mon–Thu 4pm–4am**, **Fri–Sun 3pm–4am**.
- Private rooms for up to **50 guests**, plus a public karaoke bar.
- **100k+ songs**, including thousands of international songs.

## The Copy
```
Largest private karaoke rooms in NY
Open 7 Days
Mon-Thu 4pm-4am   Fri-Sun 3pm-4am
Public karaoke bar and private karaoke rooms for up to 50 guests.
100k+ songs including thousands of international songs
```

## Watch Out
The live website currently shows **Fri–Sun opening at 4pm**, which is wrong — it should be **3pm**. That fix is listed in [[marketing-and-byob-strategy|Marketing and BYOB Strategy]], along with the room QR codes that still point at the old website.
