# Beats Karaoke — Drinks Menu

**Summary:** The full Beats Karaoke drink list, which is structured completely differently from the Christmas Karaoke menu. Beats runs on a soju and sake licence, so every cocktail and shot is built on soju or sake rather than spirits. Bottle beer runs $7–9, cocktails are a flat $12, and soju and sake bottles are $21–25.

## Key Points
- **All cocktails and shots are soju- or sake-based** — this is a beer and wine licence menu, not a full liquor one.
- Cocktails are a flat **$12** across all three sections.
- Soju is **$21 a bottle** in every flavour; sake runs **$21–25**.
- Champagne spans the widest range: **Andre $40** to **Moët & Chandon $199**.
- Guinness Draught at **$9** is the most expensive beer; Sapporo draft pitcher is **$23**.

## How This Differs From Christmas Karaoke
Christmas Karaoke sells top-shelf spirits by mix, rock and shot — see [[liquor-and-beer-price-list|Liquor, Beer and Wine Price List]]. Beats has no spirits at all; everything is rebuilt on soju and sake. That is why the same drink names appear on both menus at different prices: a Margarita is $12 here (soju-based) and $13 at Christmas Karaoke (tequila).
