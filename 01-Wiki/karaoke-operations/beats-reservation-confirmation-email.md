# Beats Karaoke — Reservation Confirmation Email

**Summary:** The Beats Karaoke version of the reservation confirmation, separate from the Christmas Karaoke one. Same $40 non-refundable deposit, but it adds a Duration field, requires every guest to bring **physical ID** because a bouncer checks at the door, and gives phone hours of 4pm to midnight.

## Key Points
- Deposit **$40**, non-refundable, deducted from the final bill.
- **All guests must bring physical identification** — the bouncer checks before entry.
- Includes a **Duration** field the Christmas version does not have.
- Phone hours: **4pm to 12am**, 718-302-3030.
- Room price depends on the size of room needed for the party.

## The Template
```
Hello
This is Beats Karaoke located at 219 Grand St, Williamsburg. Congratulations,
your reservation has been confirmed.

Name:
Reservation Date:
Reservation Time:
Number of Guests:
Duration:
Phone Number:
Deposit Amount: $40

Note:
- The price of the room depends on the size of the room it takes to accommodate
  your party.
- Deposits are non-refundable. Deposit will be deducted from your final bill.
- The card number provided at the time of reservation request will
  automatically expire.
- Make sure all guests bring their physical Identification. Bouncer will check
  it before entering the store premises.

If you have any questions about room pricing or would like to change party info,
please contact us at any time.

Call: 718-302-3030 (4pm to 12am)
Mail: Beatskaraokecafe@gmail.com

Thank you!
Beats Karaoke
```

## Difference From the Christmas Karaoke Version
| | Christmas Karaoke | Beats Karaoke |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | $40 | $40 |
| Duration field | No | **Yes** |
| ID requirement | 21+ to serve drinks in rooms | **Physical ID checked by bouncer at the door** |
| Phone hours given | No | **4pm–12am** |

The physical-ID line is worth keeping in both. It is the same fact that wins chargebacks — see [[chargeback-dispute-letter-template|Chargeback Dispute Letter]] — and telling customers up front means nobody argues about it at the door.
