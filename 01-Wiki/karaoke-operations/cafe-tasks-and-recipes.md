# Café Tasks and Recipes

**Summary:** A short set of café-side tasks and drink ratios kept separately from the bar checklists. It covers the 7pm blender and espresso machine routine, a weekly cleaning item for the coconut tub, the cold brew dilution ratio, and a note about amaranth rice crispy.

## Key Points
- At **7pm**: close 2 blenders and send them to dish.
- Clean **one side** of the espresso machine at the same time.
- Rinse the coconut tub with hot water **once a week**.
- Cold brew ratio: **2 fruit tubs concentrate to 4 fruit tubs water** (1:2).

## 7pm Routine
1. Close 2 blenders and send them to dish.
2. Clean one side of the espresso machine.
3. Collect.

## Weekly
- Rinse the coconut tub with hot water once a week.

## Cold Brew Ratio
```
2 fruit tubs  concentrate
4 fruit tubs  water
```
That is one part concentrate to two parts water.

## Full Café Closing Routine
A longer version of the close appears elsewhere in the notes:

- TV off
- Fill powder and fruit
- **Drip coffee:** Cafiza, then drain — then one more time
- Turn off the blender, remove the top cover and lower splash cover, bring to dishes
- Wash the matcha maker
- Clean the espresso grinder and take the mats
- Clean the espresso machine
- Bring back the blender tops and dishes
- Remove ice from the ice bucket
- **Pastries** go in a pizza box for the One Love donation — kept in the back of the walk-in, on the left of the stairs coming down
- Throw out trash and replace the bags
- Rinse the broth kettle
