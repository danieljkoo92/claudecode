# Café Tasks and Recipes

**Summary:** A short set of café-side tasks and drink ratios kept separately from the bar checklists. It covers the 7pm blender and espresso machine routine, a weekly cleaning item for the coconut tub, the cold brew dilution ratio, and a note about amaranth rice crispy.

## Key Points
- At **7pm**: close 2 blenders and send them to dish.
- Clean **one side** of the espresso machine at the same time.
- Rinse the coconut tub with hot water **once a week**.
- Cold brew ratio: **2 fruit tubs concentrate to 4 fruit tubs water** (1:2).

## 7pm Routine
1. Close 2 blenders and send them to dish.
2. Clean one side of the espresso machine.
3. Collect.

## Weekly
- Rinse the coconut tub with hot water once a week.

## Cold Brew Ratio
```
2 fruit tubs  concentrate
4 fruit tubs  water
```
That is one part concentrate to two parts water.
