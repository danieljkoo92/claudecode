# Christmas Karaoke — Opening Checklist

**Summary:** Step-by-step opening procedure for Christmas Karaoke. Covers turning on all systems, setting up rooms for weekday vs weekend schedules, full cleaning, bar restocking, and confirming reservations. Includes special holiday TV setup instructions and fire alarm protocol.

## Key Points
- POS, all lights, disco lights, and ATM plug must be turned on first.
- Weekdays open Korean rooms 13, 12, 11, 15 and English rooms 8 and 3. Weekends open ALL except 21 and 22.
- Only turn on the computer switch closest to the TV in each room.
- Fire alarm company must be called at 7pm to disable for 8 hours.
- Trash pickup days: Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday — throw out at 11pm.

## Step-by-Step Opening Order

1. **POS + Lights:** Turn on POS (press F1 on keyboard), all bar lights including power strip in corner, disco lights. Plug in ATM and the light plug next to it.
2. **Open Rooms:** Follow schedule above. Also turn on the outlet for room bells. Check BOTH remotes AND mics to confirm they have battery and work.
3. **TV Setup:**
   - Turn on bar TV, play music.
   - Turn on BOTH upstairs TVs.
   - **Staircase TV 1:** Power → Menu → up 2 times → Media USB → press OK on VIDEO → 1 → up 2 times → right one time → select CHRISTMAS BUBBLE → Tools → Repeat → Title.
   - **Outside TV:** Similar setup, any video. Set Repeat to ALL (not Title).
4. **Cleaning:** Sweep then mop halls, dirty rooms, and bar. Weekdays: one rag for rooms, one for bar. Weekends: use all rags. WARNING — bleach in rag water, watch out for black clothing. Hide rags if inspection arrives.
5. **Bathrooms:** Clean toilets and sinks with bleach (not Windex). Refill toilet paper — one on holder and one on the toilet.
6. **Dishes:** Move clean dishes to the bar.
7. **Garbage:** Empty all trash cans, especially food and liquid waste.
8. **Beer Fridge:** Stock and write inventory. Fill if more than 10 are missing.
9. **Bar Restock:** House liquor under 25% full → put an extra one directly behind it. Mixers under 25% → leave extra. Cut limes.
10. **Instagram:** Post daily special.
11. **Reservations:** Call all reservations for the day and confirm they are coming.
12. **Missed Calls:** Call back ALL missed calls before manager arrives.
13. **Evening:** Turn on outside 간판 (sign) and basement stair light when dark. Call fire alarm company at 7pm.
