# Closing Checklist — Master (Updated 03/04)

**Summary:** The most complete closing procedure, running from 11:30pm through final lock-up. It gives the hour-by-hour room shutdown order, how to total sales and tips, the package drink calculation, the card machine report-and-batch rule, the exact paperwork stacking and envelope order, and the full shutdown sequence. This is the version to follow when the two closing checklists disagree.

## Key Points
- **Always pull the report before batching. No exceptions.**
- Leave at least **2 rooms on and empty** at midnight in case customers arrive.
- Card sales + card tips must equal the **REVIEW** total on the machine before you batch.
- Package math: subtract **$10 per hour** from the package price, multiply by people, add to drink sales.
- Nothing may be left in the sink at the end — **not even in the strainer**.
- Paperwork goes in the closing folder in the **bar fridge**.

## 11:30 PM
- Trash nights are Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday. Trash lives under the stairs in the sprinkler room — throw it out at 11:30pm.

## 12:00 AM
- Turn off rooms **3, 10, 11**. Sweep and mop them if dirty.
- Leave at least **2 rooms on and empty** — it is still early.
- Add up sales and tips **separately** for: bar cash, room cash, room card. Write them on a small paper.
- Check: **Total room CARD sales + total room CARD tips = REVIEW** on the card machine.
- Tally every drink sold. Each drink gets its own tally except cocktails, shooters, soda and juice, which are grouped.

```
Corona      IIII II
Cocktails   IIII III
Water       III
```

## Package Drink Count and Sales
1. Count the total of every drink the packages got. Write it on the **right side** of the sales paper in **blue pen**.
2. Drinks sold normally go on the **left in black pen**. So if room 6 sold 6 Heinekens and packages got 6 Heinekens, that is 6 in black on the left and 6 in blue on the right, inside the box.
3. Subtract **$10 for every hour** in the package and add the result to drink sales.

> Example: PKG 2 × 4 people = 55 − 20 = 35 × 4 = **$140 added to drink sales** for the day.

## 1:00 AM
- Gather all trash and throw it out under the stairs.
- Clean the bathrooms with bleach, sweep and mop.
- Clean all the sinks — bar, kitchen, bathrooms.
- Turn off rooms **13 and 4**, still leaving 2 free and open.
- Add any new cash/card sales and tips to the totals you did at 12am.

## 2:00 AM
- Organize room card receipts first to last, **bill 1 on top** (if a room is staying late, do it after).
- Close the bar: do all dishes, change the caps on liquor and mixers, put everything in the fridge — **unless customers are still ordering drinks**.
- Clean the bathrooms again, all trash into the big garbage can under the stairs, wipe toilets with bleach.
- Sweep and mop. Double check for dirty rooms.
- Write the closing paper.

## 3:00 AM or 4:00 AM (depending on customers)
**ALWAYS PULL REPORT BEFORE BATCHING. NO EXCEPTIONS.**

1. Pull the report (**FUNCTION**) and match it against total CARD sales + tips. If it does not match, go through all the receipts and find the human error.
2. Once it matches, batch the sales (**FUNCTION**).
3. Take a picture of the report and batch **side by side**.
4. Staple all credit card receipts in order by bill number, with the report and batch **on top**.
5. Add all sales separately and write them on the closing paper.
6. Write total drinks sold and total drink sales on the inventory paper — sales on the bottom.
7. Take photos of: room paper, inventory paper (with drink sales), closing paper, and the AC/heater.

## Paperwork Order
Into the envelope, front to back:
1. Room card receipts and report + batch
2. Room cash
3. Bar tickets
4. Bar cash at the very back

Then stack, **bottom to top**:
1. Room paper (bottom)
2. Room itemized receipts + handwritten
3. The envelope with sales (all the way on top)

Fold the bill paper to wrap around the itemized bills and envelope, rubber band the closing paper on top, and put it in the **closing folder in the bar fridge**.

## Shutdown Sequence
- Throw out mop water **upstairs by the tree**.
- Recycle cans and bottles in the right places: Stella, Bud Light and Budweiser on the left, all others on the right, cans in the matching bags.
- Turn off the 간판 / outside light.
- Turn off the POS.
- Turn off the laptop, extension cord, and all lights except the hallway.
- Unplug the ATM and the plug on top of it.
- Close all rooms — check behind the TV for the computer power switch.
- Do all remaining dishes. **Nothing in the sink, not even in the strainer.**
- Outside Christmas tree off.
- Turn off **all** lights — nothing should be lit at all.
- Close the gate.
