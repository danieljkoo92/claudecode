# Closing Checklist v1 (Updated 03/04)

**Summary:** Timed closing procedure running from 11:30pm through 2am. Covers trash, room shutdowns, sales tallying with the card machine REVIEW check, drink count tallying, package hour math, bathroom cleaning, and writing the closing paper. This is the March 04 updated version.

## Key Points
- Always leave at least 2 rooms open and empty for late walk-in customers.
- Card sales + tip on paper must match the REVIEW number on the card machine exactly.
- Package math: subtract $10 per hour, multiply by people, add result to drink sales.
- Closing paper is the final written record for the night.
- Trash goes out at 11:30pm on Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday (under stairs, sprinkler room).

## Schedule

### 11:30 PM
- Throw out trash (under stairs in sprinkler room).

### 12:00 AM
- Turn off rooms 3, 10, 11. Sweep and mop if dirty. Leave at least 2 rooms on and empty.
- Add up all sales and tips SEPARATELY: bar cash, room cash, room card. Write on small paper.
- Verify: Total Room CARD sales + Total Room CARD tip = REVIEW on card machine.
- Tally all drinks sold. Each drink gets its own tally EXCEPT cocktails, shooters, soda, juice (lump those).

### Package Drink Count and Sales Calculation
1. Count total drinks each package received. Write on RIGHT SIDE of sales paper in BLUE PEN.
2. Black pen = actual sold count (left). Blue pen inside the box = package count (right).
3. Subtract $10 for every hour in the package, multiply by number of people, add to drink sales.
   - Example: PKG 2 × 4 people = $55 − $20 = $35 × 4 people = **$140 added to drink sales**.

### 1:00 AM
- Gather all trash and throw out under the stairs.
- Clean bathrooms with bleach — sweep and mop.
- Clean all sinks: bar, kitchen, bathrooms.
- Turn off rooms 13 and 4. Leave 2 free/open rooms.
- Add any additional cash/card sales and tips to the 12am calculations.

### 2:00 AM
- Organize room card receipts first to last (Bill 1 on top).
- Close the bar: do all dishes, cap all liquor and mixers, put everything in fridge — UNLESS customers are still ordering.
- Clean bathrooms: trash into big can under stairs, wipe toilets with bleach, sweep and mop.
- Double check for any dirty rooms.
- **Write the closing paper.**
