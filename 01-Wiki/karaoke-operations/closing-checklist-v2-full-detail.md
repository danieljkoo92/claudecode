# Closing Checklist v2 — Full Detail (April Version)

**Summary:** The most complete version of the closing checklist, running from 8pm through 3-4am. Adds photo documentation of every end-of-night document, the card machine Report and Batch process, exact stapling order, and the full closing paper format with employee hours. Use this as the master reference.

## Key Points
- Starts at 8pm — check Instagram and count both registers before anything else.
- Must photograph: room paper, inventory paper, closing paper, and AC/heater before leaving.
- Pull REPORT first, then match to totals, then BATCH — in that order. Do not batch if numbers don't match.
- Staple all card receipts by Bill # (1 on top) with Report and Batch stapled on top.
- Last person out at 3am or 4am depending on whether customers remain.

## Schedule

### 8:00 PM
- Check for Instagram post. Send all new messages to the group chat.
- Count both bar register and room register. Match money to sales.

### 12:00 AM
- Turn off rooms 3, 7, 10, 11. Sweep and mop if dirty.
- Add up all sales and tips SEPARATELY: bar cash, room cash, room card. Write on small paper.
- Verify: Total Room CARD sales + Total Room CARD tip = REVIEW on card machine.
- Tally drinks (each type separately; cocktails, shooters, soda, juice lumped together).

### 1:00 AM
- Turn off rooms 13 and 4. Leave 2 free/open rooms.
- Add additional cash/card sales and tips to 12am calculations.

### 2:00 AM
- Close bar: do all dishes, cap all liquor and mixers, put in fridge (unless customers ordering). Wipe and mop bar alcohol area and bar itself.
- **Write the closing paper.**

## Closing Paper Format
```
Christmas Karaoke
Date: 01/01/26   ← always write in this format
Bar:        sales $___   tip $___
Room cash:  sales $___   tip $___
Room card:  sales $___   tip $___
Total Sales: sales $___  tip $___
Employee hours:
  Jon:   3–4
  Jenny: 4–3
```

### 3:00–4:00 AM
1. Pull **REPORT** (FUNCTION button on card machine).
2. Match report total to your card sales + tip total. If it doesn't match, find the receipt error first.
3. Once matched, run **BATCH** (FUNCTION).
4. Take photo of Report and Batch side by side.
5. Staple ALL credit card receipts in order by Bill # with Report and Batch on top.
6. Staple bill papers in order.
7. Add all sales separately and write on closing paper.
8. Write total drinks sold and drink sales TOTAL on inventory paper (sales on bottom).
9. **Take photos:** room paper, inventory paper, closing paper, AC/heater.
