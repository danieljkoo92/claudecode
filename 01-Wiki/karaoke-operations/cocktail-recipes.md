# Cocktail Recipes

**Summary:** The full transcription of the bar's drink recipe sheet, including the handwritten additions. It covers the standard cocktail list with exact measurements, glassware and garnish, plus the non-alcoholic lemonade and simple syrup ratios and a section of drinks customers ask for that are not on the printed list.

## Key Points
- Measurements are in ounces, with glassware and garnish specified per drink.
- **Martini and Negroni are stirred, not shaken.**
- Simple syrup is a straight **1 oz sugar to 1 oz water**.
- The "Mistletoe Marg" was a handwritten addition to the sheet.
- A second section covers drinks customers ask for that are not on the main list.

## Standard List

**Baja Blast**
- 1 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Apple Pucker
- ½ oz Sweet n Sour
- Martini glass with ice
- Top with Sprite

**Blue Hawaii**
- 1 oz Rum or coconut rum
- 1 oz Blue Curaçao
- 3 oz Pineapple juice
- Cocktail glass with ice

**Cosmopolitan** *(marked EDIT on the sheet)*
- 2 oz Vodka
- ½ oz Triple Sec
- ½ oz Lime
- 1 oz Cranberry juice
- Martini glass, no ice
- Sugar rim, lime garnish

**Lychee Martini**
- 1.5 oz Lychee
- 1 oz Lime juice
- 0.5 oz Syrup
- Martini glass, sugar on rim

**Martini**
- 2 oz Gin
- 0.5 oz Dry Vermouth
- Martini glass, no ice
- Lemon twist garnish
- **Stir, don't shake**

**Margarita**
- 1.5 oz Tequila
- ¾ oz Triple Sec
- 1 oz Lime juice
- 1 oz Sweet and sour
- Cocktail glass with ice
- Salted rim, lime garnish

**Melon Ball**
- 1 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Midori
- Cocktail glass with ice
- Top with orange juice

**Mudslide**
- 1 oz Vodka
- 2 oz Kahlúa
- 1 oz Baileys
- Cocktail glass with ice

**Sex on the Beach**
- 1½ oz Vodka
- ½ oz Peach Schnapps
- 2 oz Cranberry juice
- 2 oz Orange juice
- Martini glass, no ice
- Lemon/lime garnish

**Whiskey Sour**
- 2 oz Whiskey
- 1 oz Lemon juice
- 1 oz Simple syrup
- Cocktail glass with ice
- Lemon garnish

**Strawberry Crème**
- 1.5 oz Baileys
- 1.5 oz Strawberry purée
- Cocktail glass with ice

**Bubblegum**
- 1.5 oz Baileys
- 1.5 oz Banana liqueur
- 1.5 oz Blue Curaçao
- Cocktail glass with ice

**Mistletoe Marg** *(handwritten addition)*
- 1.5 oz Tequila
- 1.5 oz Cranberry
- 0.75 oz Triple Sec
- 1 oz Lime juice
- Salted rim, cocktail glass

**Tokyo Iced Tea / AMF**
- 0.5 oz each: Vodka, Rum, Gin, Tequila, Midori, Blue Curaçao
- 1 oz Sweet & sour
- Top with Sprite

**Negroni**
- 1 oz Gin
- 1 oz Sweet Vermouth
- 1 oz Campari
- **Stir, don't shake**

## Non-Alcoholic and Mixers
**Lemonade**
- 1 oz Sugar
- 1 oz Lemon juice
- 4 oz Water

**Simple Syrup**
- 1 oz Sugar
- 1 oz Water

## Other Drinks Customers Ask For
**Tequila Sunrise** — 2 oz Tequila, top with orange juice and grenadine

**Malibu Bay Breeze** — 2 oz Malibu, top with pineapple juice and cranberry juice

**Amaretto Sour** — 2 oz Disaronno, 1 oz Simple syrup, 1 oz Lemon juice

**Dirty Shirley Temple** — recipe was not legible on the sheet

- **Spiked Popsicles** — noted separately in the source, no recipe attached.
