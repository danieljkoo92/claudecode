# Daily Sales Sheet Template

**Summary:** The standardized nightly tracking form for all karaoke room transactions. Records each room's guest count, check-in time, drink and food orders, and separates cash versus card sales with tips. The card column totals must reconcile with the card machine's REVIEW number at end of night.

## Key Points
- One row per room booking, multiple rows per page for a full night.
- Tracks room number, guest count, check-in time per booking.
- Cash sales and tips are tracked completely separately from card sales and tips.
- DEP column tracks deposits collected.
- Total card sales + card tips must match the card machine REVIEW before batching.

## Column Reference
| Column | What It Tracks |
|---|---|
| # | Booking number for the night |
| Room | Room number |
| PPL | Number of people |
| Time In | Time room started |
| Drink Order | Drinks ordered |
| Food Order | Food ordered |
| Cash Sale | Cash payment amount |
| Cash Tip | Cash tip amount |
| Card Sale | Card payment amount |
| Card Tip | Card tip amount |
| DEP | Deposit collected |

## How This Sheet Connects to End of Night
The running totals from this sheet feed directly into the [[Closing Checklist v2 — Full Detail]] process. At 12am, total card columns here and match to REVIEW on the machine. At close, these totals go onto the closing paper.
