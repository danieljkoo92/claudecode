# Daily Sales Sheet

**Summary:** The blank paper form used every night to record each room's activity. One row per booking captures the room, headcount, time in, what was ordered, and then splits payment into cash sale, cash tip, card sale, card tip and deposit. The card columns from this sheet are what get matched against the card machine's REVIEW total during closing.

## Key Points
- One row per room booking, several rows per page, dated at the top with a page number.
- Headcount and time in are recorded per room.
- Drink orders and food orders are tracked in separate columns.
- Cash and card are recorded separately, each with its own tip column.
- **DEP** column records the deposit already collected.

## Columns
| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| # | Booking number for the night |
| ROOM | Room number |
| PPL | Number of people |
| TIME / MIN — TIME IN | When the room started |
| DRINK ORDER | Drinks ordered |
| FOOD ORDER | Food ordered |
| DRINKS $ | Drink total |
| FOOD $ | Food total |
| CASH SALE $ | Cash portion of the bill |
| CASH TIP $ | Cash tip |
| CARD SALE $ | Card portion of the bill |
| CARD TIP $ | Card tip |
| DEP | Deposit collected |

## How It Feeds Closing
At midnight and again at close, the card sale and card tip columns are totalled and must equal the **REVIEW** number on the card machine. If they do not match, every receipt gets checked for the human error before batching. The full procedure is in [[closing-checklist-master|Closing Checklist — Master]].
