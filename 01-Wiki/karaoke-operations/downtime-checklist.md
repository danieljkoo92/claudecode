# Downtime Checklist

**Summary:** Tasks for staff to complete during slow periods at the karaoke bar. Split into deep cleaning duties that go beyond the standard opening and closing checklists, plus social media and business development tasks. Everything on this list should be done whenever the store is not busy.

## Key Points
- Deep clean every sink and toilet — nothing visible anywhere.
- Clean all glass surfaces: room door windows, soju Christmas tree doors, mirrors.
- Organize the POS area into three specific zones.
- Like and comment on Instagram accounts we follow.
- Find 5 big Long Island companies to target for corporate marketing emails.

## Deep Cleaning
- Double check inspection checklist (see [[Health Inspection Checklist]]).
- Clean every single sink.
- Clean every single toilet — nothing visible.
- Wipe ALL dirty tables and mop ALL dirty rooms.
- Wipe refrigerator and ice machine.
- Clean behind ALL TVs.
- Mop bar.
- Sweep AND mop stairs.
- Organize POS area: (1) trash can area, (2) left of cash register, (3) behind mic chargers.
- Zero visible trash on floor anywhere in the store.
- Sweep and mop kitchen if dirty.
- Clean all visible glass: room door windows, soju Christmas tree doors, mirrors, etc.

## Marketing Tasks
- Like and comment on accounts we follow on the main Instagram.
- Find a list of 5 big companies in Long Island for corporate marketing emails.
