# Downtime Checklist

**Summary:** What staff should be doing when the store is slow. It is a deep-clean list that goes further than the nightly close — every sink and toilet, behind the TVs, the POS area, and all the glass — plus two marketing tasks: engaging on Instagram and building a corporate email list.

## Key Points
- Start by double checking the inspection checklist.
- Nothing visible in any toilet, and no visible trash anywhere on the floor.
- The POS area gets organized in three specific spots.
- Instagram engagement: like and comment on accounts the store follows.
- Find **5 big companies in Long Island** for corporate marketing emails.

## Deep Cleaning
- Double check the inspection checklist — see [[health-inspection-checklist|Health Inspection Checklist]].
- Clean every single sink.
- Clean every single toilet — nothing visible.
- Wipe all dirty tables and mop all dirty rooms.
- Wipe the refrigerator and ice machine.
- Clean behind **all** the TVs.
- Mop the bar.
- Sweep and mop the stairs.
- Sweep and mop the kitchen if dirty.
- No visible trash on the floor throughout the whole store.
- Clean all visible glass: the room door windows, the doors by the soju Christmas trees, mirrors, etc.

## POS Area
Organize these three spots:
1. Where the trash can is
2. Left of the cash register
3. Behind the mic chargers

## Marketing
- Like and comment on people the store follows from the main Instagram account.
- Find a list of **5 big companies in Long Island** to send corporate marketing emails to.
