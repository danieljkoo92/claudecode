# Front Desk Shift Notes

**Summary:** Working notes for whoever is on the front desk — what to ask guests when they arrive, the POS access code, the rule on underage guests, the happy hour minimum, weekly ordering reminders, and which rooms to open. It also carries the room capacity chart, which is the fastest reference for seating a party.

## Key Points
- Ask every guest: **age, name, gender, where they're from and where they live.**
- POS code: **GKmspos — ALWAYS 2018**.
- **Underage is always prepaid.** No exceptions.
- Happy hour is **6 per person, minimum 3 people**.
- Order alcohol on **Monday**, and add a note to deliver at **3pm on Thursday**.
- Open rooms **1+2 and 4–8**.

## Guest Intake
Ask age, name, gender, where they are from and where they live.

## Standing Rules
- **TEMP POS** — GKmspos, always 2018.
- Underage always prepaid.
- Light next to the ATM.
- Happy hour: 6 per person, minimum 3 people.
