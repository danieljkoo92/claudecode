# Health Inspection Checklist

**Summary:** Emergency protocol for when a health inspector arrives. Covers mandatory certifications, the three sinks that must be empty, hand-washing station requirements, the rag ban, required signage in every room, ice machine standards, refrigerator maintenance, and the 10-inch floor clearance rule for all food and drink.

## Key Points
- Food handler certificate is MANDATORY — every staff member on shift needs one.
- ALL trash cans must have lids.
- Zero items in any of the three sinks — not even a straw or a piece of lime.
- All hand soaps must be at least 70% full.
- No rags during inspection — paper towels only. Hide all rags immediately.
- All drinks, liquor, and food must be 10 inches above the floor.

## Immediate Actions When Inspector Arrives
1. Confirm food handler certificates are present for all staff on shift.
2. Double check closing shift's work: bathrooms, trash cans (lids on all), room cleanliness.
3. Check all three sinks: (1) Kitchen sink, (2) Bar main sink, (3) Bar hand-washing sink at end of bar. Nothing in any of them.
4. Check UNDER all sinks — no water. If there is water, mop it immediately.
5. Confirm paper towels are at EVERY hand-washing sink.
6. Confirm all hand soaps are at least 70% full (1 kitchen, 2 in each bathroom, both bar sinks).
7. **Hide all rags immediately.** Only paper towels when inspectors are present.
8. Confirm NO SMOKING and CHOKING HAZARD signs are posted properly in every room.

## Equipment Standards
- **Ice machine:** Remove enough ice to use, then clean with a rag and toothbrush for crevices. Nothing black, yellow, or brown allowed inside.
- **Refrigerator interior:** Check bottom for water — clean immediately if found.
- **Refrigerator exterior:** Clean outside including bottom front (inspectors check for warmth that attracts insects and also deduct for dirty exteriors).
- **Storage height:** All drinks, liquor, and food stored 10 inches ABOVE the floor. Use plastic boxes if needed.

## Required Signage
- "Employees must wash hands" sign in every bathroom.
- No Smoking sign in every room.
- Choking Hazard sign in every room.

## Other
- Check PHP water test kit.
