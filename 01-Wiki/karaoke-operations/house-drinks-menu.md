# House Drinks Menu

**Summary:** The customer-facing drinks menu with house cocktails, shooters, sodas and juices, plus the prices for pitchers and upgrades. Cocktails are $13 and shooters $10, with Long Island and Tokyo Iced Tea at $16 and a $3 top-shelf substitution. Three cocktails carry Christmas names — Grinch's, Santa's and Frosty's.

## Key Points
- **Cocktails $13**, **shooters $10**.
- Long Island and Tokyo Iced Tea are **$16**; Jagerbomb **$12**; Redbull **$6**.
- **Top shelf substitution +$3**.
- Soda: glass $2.50, pitcher $12. Juice: glass $3.50, pitcher $15.
- Three drinks have holiday branding: **(Grinch's) Baja Blast**, **(Santa's) Strawberry Crème**, **(Frosty's) Bubblegum**.
