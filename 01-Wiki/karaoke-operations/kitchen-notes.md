# Kitchen Notes

**Summary:** Two short kitchen rules about fryer order and timing. If chicken and fries are ordered together, the fries go in first, because chicken drops the oil temperature and leaves the fries taking far too long.

## Key Points
- **Always cook fries first** when chicken and fries are ordered at the same time.
- Chicken lowers the oil temperature, which makes fries take forever.
- **Chicken: 3 minutes.**

## The Rules
1. If chicken and fries are ordered at the same time, always cook the fries first.
2. Chicken lowers the temperature of the oil, and fries then take far too long to cook.
3. Chicken cooks in 3 minutes.
