# Liquor, Beer and Wine Price List

**Summary:** The full drink price list covering top shelf spirits, house liquor, soju, beer, wine and mocktails. Spirits are priced three ways — mix, rock/neat, and shot — with Casamigos at the top and a flat house rate underneath. This is the companion to the cocktail menu and the pricing structure the POS app is being designed around.

## Key Points
- Every spirit has **three prices**: mix, rock/neat, and shot.
- **House liquor is flat**: mix $11, rock/neat $10, shot $8.
- **Casamigos** is the most expensive at $16 / $16 / $13.
- **Soju $15**, **mocktails $6**, **wine bottle $45**.
- Beers are **$7 or $8** — the domestic three at $7, everything else $8.

## Why the Three-Price Structure Matters
This is exactly the menu problem described in [[pos-app-feature-list|POS App — Feature List]]: house and top shelf each have three categories at three prices, and listing every combination separately would mean a thousand menu items. The POS needs sub-options per item instead.
