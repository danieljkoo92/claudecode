# Reservation Confirmation Email Template

**Summary:** Standard confirmation email sent to customers after a reservation is booked at Christmas Karaoke. Includes the $40 deposit policy, age requirement for drink service, weekend pricing note, and contact details. Use this template for every outgoing reservation confirmation.

## Key Points
- Deposit is $40, non-refundable, deducted from the final bill.
- Everyone in the room must be 21+ for drinks to be served, unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.
- Weekend room pricing may vary based on how large a room is needed.
- The card number used to reserve automatically expires — it is not stored.

## Email Template

```
Hello,

This is Christmas Karaoke.

Congratulations, your reservation has been confirmed.

Name:
Reservation Date:
Reservation Time:
Number of Guests:
Phone Number:
Deposit Amount: $40

Note:
- On weekends the price of the room may depend on the size of the room
  it takes to accommodate your party.
- Deposits are non-refundable. Deposit will be deducted from your final bill.
- The card number provided at the time of reservation request will
  automatically expire.
- We require everyone in the room to be 21 to serve drinks into the rooms
  unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.

If you have any questions about room pricing or would like to change party
info, please contact us at any time.

Phone: 718-224-2434
Email: info@karaokexmas@gmail.com
```
