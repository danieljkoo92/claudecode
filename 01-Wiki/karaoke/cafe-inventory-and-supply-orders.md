# Café Inventory and Supply Orders

**Summary:** Two related counts kept for the café side — a tally of smoothie ingredients and retail items with some prices, and a supply order sheet listing cup sizes, lids, straws and supplement powders with quantities. The numbers are written in shorthand, so the interpretations below are flagged where they are uncertain.

## Key Points
- Smoothie ingredients are counted in tally marks (I, II, III), not numbers.
- **Coco meat** is the most stocked ingredient at four.
- Supplement powders are ordered by the gram or unit — **Defense 3080** is the largest line.
- Cup sizes tracked: 8oz, 12oz and 16oz, hot and cold, each with its own lid count.
- The number format is inconsistent, so confirm against the actual order sheet before ordering.
