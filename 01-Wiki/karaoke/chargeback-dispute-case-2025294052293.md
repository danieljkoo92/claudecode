## The Pattern Across All Three Disputes
This is the third case in the notes, and the same structure appears every time:

| Case | Amount | Bill before discount | Discount |

| **2025294052293** | $318.89 | $529.12 | $210.23 |

Two of the three involve a large discount being applied at checkout and then disputed afterward. That is worth noticing: **a discounted bill is more likely to be charged back**, probably because the final number does not match what the customer expected to see. Writing the discount clearly on the itemized receipt — and pointing at it when handing the receipt over — would make these disputes even easier to win, or stop them being filed.
