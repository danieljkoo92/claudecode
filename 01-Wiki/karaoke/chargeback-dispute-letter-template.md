# Chargeback Dispute Letter — Blank Template

**Summary:** A reusable fill-in-the-blanks chargeback response letter, with bracketed fields for the customer name, transaction details and service provided. It follows the same structure that worked in both real disputes: state the transaction was legitimate and fulfilled, list the supporting evidence, note that ID and signature were required, and confirm no complaint was made at the time.

## Key Points
- Blank template — every variable is in `[brackets]`.
- The core argument: the transaction was **legitimate, authorized, and fulfilled as agreed**.
- Always states that **valid ID and signature are required** for private room bookings.
- Always states that **no complaint or refund request** was made at the time or afterward.
- Attaches: original receipt, signed agreement/check-in form, booking communications.

## The Template
```
[Daniel Choi / Assistant Manager]
Beats Karaoke
219 Grand Street
Brooklyn 11211
718-302-3030
Beatskaraokecafe@gmail.com
[Date]

To: Chargeback Department
Subject: Chargeback Dispute for [Case]

Dear Chargeback Department,

I am writing to formally dispute a chargeback claim filed by [Customer's Name]
on [Transaction Date] in the amount of $[Amount], related to a purchase made at
[Venue Name], a karaoke lounge located in Brooklyn, NY.

This chargeback is invalid because the transaction was legitimate, authorized,
and fulfilled as agreed upon. The customer received the full service purchased —
karaoke room access and associated amenities — on the date of service.

Supporting Details:
• Transaction ID: [Transaction ID]
• Date & Time of Service: [Date & Time]
• Authorized Cardholder Name: [Name on Card]
• Amount: $[Amount]
• Receipt/Invoice Number: [Invoice or POS Number]
• Service Provided: [e.g., Private Room Booking for 2 Hours + Drinks]
• Proof of Service: (Attach signed receipt, room check-in sheet, ID
  verification, or security footage if applicable)

Our establishment requires valid identification and signature for all private
room bookings, and the customer in question signed and paid in person using the
authorized card. There were no complaints or refund requests filed at the time
of service or afterward.

We kindly request that you reverse the chargeback and release the disputed funds
back to our account. Please find attached the supporting evidence for this
transaction, including:
• The original sales receipt
• Copy of the signed agreement / check-in form
• Communication logs or messages confirming the booking

Should you require any additional documentation or clarification, please contact
me directly at 718-302-3030 or Beatskaraokecafe@gmail.com

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

Sincerely,
Daniel Choi
Manager
Beats Karaoke Cafe
```

## Watch Out
The saved draft names **"233 Starr Karaoke & Eats"** in the body while the letterhead says Beats Karaoke — a leftover from whichever version it was adapted from. Change the venue name every time or the letter contradicts itself, which is exactly the kind of thing a reviewer notices.

## What Actually Won the Real Cases
Both real disputes were won on the same two facts, so make sure they appear every time:
1. The **itemized receipt was shown before charging**.
2. **ID was verified against the card.**

See [[chargeback-dispute-case-2026083002682|the Christmas Karaoke case]], which came back reversed, and [[chargeback-dispute-case-202528100557|the Beats case]].
