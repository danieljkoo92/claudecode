# Christmas Karaoke — Opening Checklist

**Summary:** The full opening routine for Christmas Karaoke, in the order staff should work through it. It covers powering up the POS and lights, which rooms to open on weekdays versus weekends, the Christmas video setup on the upstairs TVs, cleaning, restocking the bar and beer fridge, and the phone and Instagram tasks that must be done before the manager arrives. It ends with the evening items: outside sign, fire alarm call, and trash nights.

## Key Points
- Turn on the POS with **F1**, then all bar lights, the corner power strip, the disco lights, the ATM plug and the light plug beside it.
- Weekdays open Korean rooms **13, 12, 11, 15** and English rooms **8, 3**, then **4, 5**. Weekends open **everything except 21 and 22**.
- In each room only turn on the computer switch closest to the TV.
- Call the fire alarm company at **7pm** to disable it for 8 hours.
- Trash goes out at **11pm on Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday**.
- No missed calls should be left when the manager arrives.

## Power and Rooms
1. Turn on POS (press F1) and all lights by the bar, including the power strip in the corner and the disco lights next to the house liquor.
2. Plug in the ATM plug and the light plug next to it.
3. Open the rooms for the day. Turn on the outlet for the room bells.
4. Check **both remotes and mics** in each room — confirm they have battery and actually work.

## TV Setup
- Turn on the bar TV and play music.
- Turn on **both** upstairs TVs.

**Staircase TV 1:**
1. Power.
2. Menu → up ×2 → Media USB → OK on VIDEO → 1 → up ×2 → right ×1 → **Christmas Bubble**.
3. Tools → Repeat → **Title**.

**Outside TV:** any video, set up the same way, except Repeat is **All**, not Title.

## Cleaning
- Sweep, then mop the halls, any dirty rooms, and the bar.
- Weekdays: rinse one rag for the rooms and one for the bar. Weekends: all rags.
- **Warning — there is bleach in the rag water. Watch out for black clothes.**
- If an inspection arrives, hide the rags (see [[health-inspection-checklist|Health Inspection Checklist]]).
- Clean the bathrooms — toilets and sinks with **bleach, not Windex**. Refill toilet paper: one on the holder and one on the toilet.

## Stocking
- Move clean dishes to the bar.
- Empty all garbage, especially food and liquids.
- Stock the beer fridge and write the inventory. Fill if more than 10 are missing.
- Restock the bar:
  - Any house liquor under 25% full — put an extra bottle directly behind it.
  - Mixers under 25% full — leave an extra one out.
  - Cut limes.

## Phone and Social
- Post the daily special on Instagram.
- Call every reservation for that day and confirm they are coming.
- Call back **all** missed calls — none should be showing when the manager gets to the store.

## Evening
- Turn on the outside 간판 (sign) and the basement stair light when it gets dark.
- **7pm:** call the fire alarm company and disable for 8 hours.
- Trash nights are Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday — out at 11pm.

## Steps From Other Versions
Two more versions of this checklist exist in the big notes file. They mostly match, but carry a few steps the printed one does not:

- **Bar TV audio:** press **output 1 on the amp** and switch the laptop output to **headphones**.
- **5pm:** turn on the outside 간판 (rather than just "when dark").
- **Trash:** gather all the store's trash into **one bag** and put it under the stairs.
- **Beer fridge:** write the **full** inventory — count the beer and drinks and write it down, rather than only topping up when 10+ are missing.
- **Rags:** one version says rinse **2** rags on weekdays instead of 1.
- **Room list variant:** weekdays Korean 13, 12, 11, 15 and English 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, with **all** rooms on weekends.

Where they disagree, the printed checklist above is the one that was filed on its own, so treat it as current and these as history.
