# Closing Checklist — April Version

**Summary:** A second closing checklist that starts earlier in the evening than the master version. It adds an 8pm step for Instagram and counting both registers, and it asks the closer to write their own ending time on the paperwork. The overnight steps largely match the master checklist but are shorter on the package math and paperwork stacking, so use the master version when the two differ.

## Key Points
- Starts at **8pm**, earlier than the master checklist.
- Both registers — bar and room — get counted and matched to sales at 8pm.
- Room shutdown at midnight here is **3, 7, 10, 11** (the master version says 3, 10, 11).
- The closer writes their **own ending time** on the paperwork.
- Closing paper is dated in the format **01/01/26**.

## 8:00 PM
- Check for the Instagram post and send all new messages to the group chat.
- Count both the bar and room registers and match the money to sales.

## 12:00 AM
- Turn off rooms **3, 7, 10, 11**. Sweep and mop if dirty.
- Add up sales and tips **separately** for bar cash, room cash, room card. Write on a small paper.
- Check that room card sales + tips match the **REVIEW** on the card machine.
- Tally all drinks sold — each drink separately except cocktails, shooters, soda and juice.

## 1:00 AM
- Turn off rooms **13 and 4**, leaving 2 free and open.
- Add any additional cash/card sales and tips to the 12am totals.

## 2:00 AM
- Close the bar: do all dishes, change caps on liquor and mixers, put everything in the fridge — unless customers are still ordering.
- Wipe and mop the bar alcohol area and the bar itself.
- Write the closing paper.

Christmas Karaoke

## 3:00 AM or 4:00 AM (depending on customers)
1. Pull the report (**FUNCTION**) and match it with total card sales + tips. If it does not match, go through all receipts and find the human error.
2. Once it matches, batch (**FUNCTION**).
3. Take a picture of the report and batch side by side.
4. Staple all credit card receipts in order by bill number, with report and batch on top.
5. Staple bill papers in order.
6. Add all sales separately and write on the closing paper.
7. Write total drinks sold and total drink sales on the inventory paper, sales on the bottom.
8. Photos: room paper, inventory paper, closing paper, AC/heater.
9. Write **Christmas Karaoke** and the opening date.

## Paperwork Order
Into the envelope, front to back: credit card receipts, then room cash, then bar tickets, with bar cash at the back.

Stack in this order: room paper, then bill paper, then the envelope. Fold it, wrap the closing paper around it, rubber band it, and put it in the closing folder.

## Shutdown Sequence
- Throw out mop water upstairs by the tree.
- Recycle cans and bottles in the right spots — Stella, Bud Light, Budweiser on the left, all others on the right, cans in matching bags.
- Turn off the 간판 / outside light.
- Turn off the POS.
- Turn off the laptop, extension cord and all lights except the hallway.
- Unplug the ATM and the plug on top.
- Close all rooms — check behind the TV for the computer power switch.
- Do all remaining dishes.
- **Write your ending time.**
- Outside Christmas tree off.
- Turn off all lights — nothing lit at all.
- Close the gate.
