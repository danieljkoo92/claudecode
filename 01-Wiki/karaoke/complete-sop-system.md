# Complete SOP System (Version 1)

**Summary:** A full standard-operating-procedure manual covering every part of running the venue — greeting walk-ins, explaining pricing, assigning rooms, turning rooms over, handling drinks and intoxication, billing, cleaning, shift changes, marketing moments and emergencies. Each SOP states its purpose, the steps, and the mistakes to avoid. This is the single most useful document for training a new hire.

## Key Points
- **Greet within 3 seconds** unless it would interrupt a conversation.
- **Pricing explanation is the critical SOP** — it exists specifically to eliminate disputes and bad reviews, and it ends with getting a **verbal confirmation**.
- **Room turnover target: under 5 minutes.**
- Equipment problems must be **fixed within 2–3 minutes** or the guest gets moved.
- Bathroom checks **every 30–60 minutes**.
- Over-assigning big rooms is flagged as a mistake that **kills revenue**.

## 1. Front Desk / Customer Flow

### SOP: Greeting Walk-In Customers
**Purpose:** Set tone, check ID, control pricing expectations.
**When:** Guests arrive. **Who:** Everyone.

1. Greet within 3 seconds unless you would be interrupting a conversation.
2. Ask: does someone in your party have a reservation?
3. Ask: "How many people?"
4. Ask: "How long are you planning to sing?"
5. Check room availability.
6. Transition into the pricing explanation.

**Standards:** No ignoring customers. No casual or sloppy tone. Always lead the conversation.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Greeted immediately
- [ ] Group size and ETA for incomplete parties
- [ ] Take an ID and card to keep on file — explain nothing will be charged
- [ ] Time expectation asked

### SOP: Pricing Explanation (CRITICAL)
**Purpose:** Eliminate disputes and bad reviews.

1. State price per person per hour, **or** the room rate for VIP rooms.
2. Mention current packages.
3. Clarify that billing is time-based.
4. Ask: "Does that sound good?"
5. **Wait for verbal confirmation.**

**VIP room rates as given here:**
| Room | Standard | Happy hour |
|---|---|---|
| #1 | $250/hr | $150/hr |
| #6 | $200/hr | $120/hr |
*(plus tax and tip)*

**Rules:** No assumptions. No skipping steps.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Price explained
- [ ] Minimum explained
- [ ] Promo explained
- [ ] Customer confirmed

### SOP: Room Assignment
1. Match group size to the correct room.
2. Avoid giving oversized rooms unless necessary.
3. Prioritize filling smaller rooms first.
4. Confirm equipment is working before seating.

Room 1 capacity noted: minimum 15, up to 25–30 seated, 40–50 including standing.

**Mistakes to avoid:** Over-assigning big rooms (**kills revenue**). Sending guests to untested rooms.

### SOP: Starting a Session
1. Escort the group to the room.
2. Turn on the system.
3. Test mic and music.
4. Explain how to use the system.
5. **Start the timer immediately.**

## 2. Room Operations

### SOP: Room Turnover (VERY HIGH IMPACT)
**Goal: reset the room in under 5 minutes.**

1. Remove all trash
2. Wipe tables and surfaces
3. Check floor
4. Sanitize mics
5. Reset system
6. Quick smell check

**Standards:** No visible mess. No sticky surfaces. No odor.

**Checklist:** ☐ Trash removed ☐ Surfaces cleaned ☐ Mics sanitized ☐ System reset

### SOP: Equipment Issue (Mic / Audio)
1. Check if muted
2. Swap mic
3. Restart system
4. Check cables
5. Move guest if unresolved

**Rule: fix within 2–3 minutes maximum.**

### SOP: Lost Items
1. Log the item immediately
2. Label with date and room
3. Store in lost & found
4. If high value, notify the manager

## 3. Bar / Drinks

### SOP: Drink Order Handling
1. Take the full order clearly
2. **Repeat the order back**
3. Enter or remember accurately
4. Deliver quickly

### SOP: BOGO (Tuesday Control)
1. Confirm eligible drinks
2. Apply BOGO correctly
3. Track total drinks per group

**Mistakes:** Giving extra free drinks. Not tracking.

### SOP: Intoxication Control
1. Watch behaviour — slurring, aggression
2. Slow service
3. Switch to water or suggest food
4. Stop service if needed
5. Notify the manager

---

## 4. Billing (MOST IMPORTANT SYSTEM)

### SOP: Final Bill Calculation
1. Calculate room total
2. Add drinks and items
3. Apply tax (**8.875%**)
4. Apply gratuity (**19%**)
5. Calculate card fee (**3% of subtotal**)

**Always show both the cash total and the card total.**

### SOP: Checkout Process
1. Present the bill clearly
2. Explain the breakdown if needed
3. Handle split payments
4. Process payment
5. Thank the customer

### SOP: Bill Disputes
1. Stay calm
2. Re-explain pricing
3. Show the breakdown
4. Offer a minor resolution if reasonable
5. Escalate if needed

**Never:** argue emotionally, or blame the customer.

---

## 5. Cleaning

### SOP: Bathroom Check
**Frequency: every 30–60 minutes.**
Check toilet · refill paper · wipe surfaces · check smell

### SOP: Odor Control
1. Identify the source
2. Remove trash immediately
3. Air out the room
4. Use neutralizer

---

## 6. Employee

### SOP: Shift Start
Clock in · check assigned area · review notes from the previous shift · confirm readiness

### SOP: Shift Handoff
Update the next staff member on active rooms, issues and pending bills. Clean your workstation.

---

## 7. Marketing

### SOP: Asking for Reviews
Ask after a positive moment. Offer an incentive if applicable. Be natural, not pushy.

### SOP: Birthday Handling
Verify ID · offer promo if applicable · note in system · enhance the experience

---

## 8. Emergency

### SOP: Fight / Aggressive Customer
1. **Do not engage physically**
2. Separate the parties
3. Call the manager
4. Call police if needed

### SOP: System Failure
1. Restart the system
2. Move guests if needed
3. Offer compensation if there is a delay

This SOP — state the price, explain the breakdown, **wait for verbal confirmation** — is the procedure that prevents them. See [[chargeback-dispute-letter-template|Chargeback Dispute Letter]].
