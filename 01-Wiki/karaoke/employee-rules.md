# Employee Rules

**Summary:** Seven house rules for staff, covering lateness, friends at work, tips, drinking on the job, scheduling, notice periods, and who to ask when something is unclear. Short and blunt — this is the list to hand a new hire on day one.

## Key Points
- **No individual tips** — tips are pooled, not kept personally.
- **No drinking on the job.**
- **No friends** at work.
- Staff manage their own schedule: **2 weeks notice** and find your own cover first.
- **Two weeks notice** before quitting.
- Questions go to the **manager**, not to other employees.

## The Rules
1. Don't be late.
2. No friends.
3. No individual tip.
4. No drinking on the job.
5. Responsible for your own schedule. At least 2 weeks notice and ask for cover first.
6. Two weeks notice before quitting.
7. Ask the manager if there's any confusion or questions — don't ask employees. They don't [know].

## Worth Noting
Rule 3 — no individual tips — is the same fact that anchors the comp argument in [[manager-argument-the-numbers|Manager Argument — The Numbers]]: since gratuity is charged and pooled, a server giving away a free drink gains nothing personally from it. That is what makes comping a marketing decision rather than a self-serving one.

Rule 7 has an unfinished sentence in the source — it cuts off at "They don't". The meaning is clear enough from context.
