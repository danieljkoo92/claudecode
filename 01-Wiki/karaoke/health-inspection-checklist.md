# Health Inspection Checklist

**Summary:** What to fix immediately when a health inspection is happening or expected. It covers the mandatory food handler certificate, the three sinks that must be completely empty, hand soap and paper towel requirements, the rule about hiding rags, required signage, and cleaning standards for the ice machine and refrigerator. It also notes the 10-inch floor clearance rule for anything drinkable or edible.

## Key Points
- **Food handler certificate is mandatory.**
- **All trash cans must have lids.**
- Absolutely zero items in any sink — not even a straw or a piece of lime.
- No water under any sink. If you see water, mop it immediately.
- **We do not use rags** if inspectors ask — paper towels only. Hide all rags immediately.
- All drinks, liquor and food must sit **10 inches above the floor**.

## Immediate Checks
1. Food handler certificate — mandatory.
2. Double check the closing shift's work: bathrooms, trash cans (all must have lids), room cleanliness.
3. The three sinks must be completely empty:
   - Kitchen sink
   - Bar main sink
   - Bar hand washing sink at the end of the bar
4. Under all sinks must be dry — mop immediately if not.
5. Paper towel by **every** hand washing sink.
6. All hand soaps at least **70% full** — 1 kitchen, 2 in each bathroom, both bar sinks.
7. Hide all rags. Paper towels only.
8. **No Smoking** and **Choking Hazard** signs posted properly in every room.
9. "Employees must wash hands" sign in the bathrooms.
10. Check the PHP water test kit.

## Ice Machine
1. Take out enough ice to use.
2. Clean with a rag and a toothbrush for the crevices.
3. Nothing black, yellow or brown.

## Refrigerator
- Check inside the bottom for water. If there is any, clean it immediately.
- Clean the outside including the bottom front — inspectors look for warm places where insects gather, and they also give points for the refrigerator simply being dirty.

## Storage
All drinks, liquor and food must be **10 inches above the floor**. Use a plastic box if needed.
