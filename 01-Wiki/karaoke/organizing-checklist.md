# Organizing Checklist

**Summary:** A short six-item tidying list, notable for the warning attached to the under-stairs storage: that space is not ours, and if it stays messy the landlord could tell us to stop using it.

## Key Points
- **The under-stairs space is not ours.** If it looks messy the landlord could revoke it.
- Count cans as step one.
- Kitchen dishes go back to the kitchen, then clean the kitchen.
- Sweep the stairs and outside if dirty.

## The List
1. Count cans.
2. Organize under the stairs. **Under the stairs is not ours — it's messy and the landlord could tell us to stop using it.**
3. Organize the POS area.
4. Kitchen dishes to the kitchen.
5. Clean the kitchen.
6. Sweep the stairs and outside if dirty.

## Why the Under-Stairs Warning Matters
The closing procedure depends on that space — trash goes under the stairs at 11:30pm and again at 1am, and the recycling is sorted there. Losing it would break the whole close. See [[closing-checklist-master|Closing Checklist — Master]].
