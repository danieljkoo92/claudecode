# Reservation Confirmation Email

**Summary:** The template sent to customers once a reservation at Christmas Karaoke is confirmed. It captures the party details, states the $40 deposit and that it is non-refundable but applied to the final bill, warns that weekend room pricing depends on party size, and sets the 21+ rule for drinks in the rooms.

## Key Points
- Deposit is **$40**, non-refundable, deducted from the final bill.
- Weekend room price can depend on the size of room needed for the party.
- The card number given at reservation time automatically expires.
- Everyone in the room must be **21** for drinks to be served, unless with a parent or guardian.
- Contact: **718-224-2434** / info@karaokexmas@gmail.com

## Template
```
Hello
This is Christmas Karaoke.
Congratulations, your reservation has been confirmed.

Name:
Reservation Date:
Reservation Time:
Number of Guests:
Phone Number:
Deposit Amount: $40

Note:
- On weekends the price of the room may depend on the size of the room it takes
  to accommodate your party.
- Deposits are non-refundable. Deposit will be deducted from your final bill.
- The card number provided at the time of reservation request will automatically expire.
- We require everyone in the room to be 21 to serve drinks into the rooms unless
  accompanied by a parent or guardian.

If you have any questions about room pricing or would like to change party info,
please contact us at any time.

Phone: 7182242434
Email: info@karaokexmas@gmail.com
```
