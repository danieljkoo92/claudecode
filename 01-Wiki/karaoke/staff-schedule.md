# Staff Schedule

**Summary:** A weekly shift pattern for Daniel and Justin. Daniel opens most days at 4pm and hands over to Justin at 10pm; Sunday Daniel works 3pm to close, and Tuesday is a single shift 4pm to close.

## Key Points
- **Daniel opens** most days at 4pm, **Justin closes** from 10pm.
- **Sunday:** Daniel works 3 to close — the longest single shift.
- **Tuesday:** one shift, 4 to close, no handover.
- Wednesday through Saturday all follow the same 4–10 / 10–close split.

## The Schedule
| Day | Shift |
|---|---|
| Sunday | Daniel 3 – close |
| Monday | Daniel 4–10, Justin 10 – close |
| Tuesday | 4 – close |
| Wednesday | Daniel 4–10, Justin 10 – close |
| Thursday | Same |
| Friday | Same |
| Saturday | Same |
