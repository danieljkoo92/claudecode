# Waste and Recycling Contract

**Summary:** Terms noted for the waste and recycling service. The monthly cost is $120 — a $110 base plus a $10 recycling fee — on a two-year contract rather than the five-year one originally proposed, and it cancels if the business moves. Payment is not accepted in cash.

## Key Points
- **$110 + $10 recycle fee = $120** total.
- **Two-year contract**, not the five-year version first offered.
- **Cancels if we move** — no penalty for relocating.
- **No cash payment** accepted.

## The Terms
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Base fee | $110 |
| Recycling fee | $10 |
| **Total** | **$120** |
| Contract length | 2 years (not 5) |
| If the business moves | Contract is cancelled |
| Payment | No cash |

## Why the Two Changes Matter
Getting the term down from five years to two, and getting a cancellation-on-move clause, are both worth more than the price. A five-year waste contract that survives a relocation is the kind of thing that quietly costs thousands when a lease ends.
