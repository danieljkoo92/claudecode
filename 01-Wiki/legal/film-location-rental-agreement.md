# Film Location Rental Agreement — Heart Productions

**Summary:** A complete invoice and rental contract for hiring out Christmas Karaoke as a filming location for a day. The deal is $2,000 for exclusive use from 6am to 2pm on Friday 6 February 2026, with a 20% non-refundable deposit. The contract covers use of premises, damages, liability, indemnification, cancellation and governing law.

## Key Points
- **$2,000** for exclusive use, 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
- **$400 deposit (20%)** on signing, **$1,600** balance due on or before the event date.
- Deposit is **non-refundable** once paid.
- Renter carries **full responsibility** for personnel, equipment and activities, and indemnifies the venue.
- Governed by **New York** law.
- Client: **Heart Productions** — Brandon Denny, (631) 352-9714, brandondenny33@gmail.com

## Why This Matters
The booking runs **6am to 2pm**, which is entirely outside operating hours — the venue opens at 3–4pm. This is $2,000 for a room that would otherwise be empty and unstaffed, with no bar cost, no cleaning cycle beyond the usual, and no lost bookings. Against a $10,000/week revenue goal, one filming day is a fifth of the week's target earned before opening.

Worth treating as a repeatable product rather than a one-off, and worth listing the space on location-scouting platforms.

## The Contract Terms

**2. Date & Time of Use** — Friday, February 6, 2026, 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Purpose: filming a movie, video, or related production content.

**3. Rental Fee** — $2,000 total. $400 (20%) deposit on signing to reserve the date. $1,600 balance due prior to or on the event date. Failure to pay the balance may result in cancellation without refund of deposit.

**4. Use of Premises** — Premises may be used solely for the filming and production activities agreed in advance. No unlawful, hazardous or unauthorized activities.

**5. Damages** — Renter leaves the premises in the same condition as received. Any damage caused by renter, crew, cast or equipment is repaired at renter's expense.

**6. Insurance & Liability** — Renter assumes full responsibility for all personnel, equipment and activities during the rental. Owner is not liable for injury, loss or damage arising from renter's use.

**7. Indemnification** — Renter indemnifies and holds Christmas Karaoke harmless from claims, liabilities, damages or expenses arising from their use of the premises.

**8. Cancellation** — Deposit non-refundable unless cancellation is agreed in writing by both parties. Owner may cancel for safety, legal or operational reasons, refunding payments received.

**9. Governing Law** — New York State.

**10. Entire Agreement** — Constitutes the entire agreement; modifiable only in writing signed by both parties.

Signature blocks for Owner and Renter / Production Company.

## Before Signing the Next One
- **Ask for a certificate of insurance.** Clause 6 assigns the renter full responsibility, but that only means something if they actually carry production insurance — request the COI naming the venue as additional insured.
- The email on the invoice is written as `Info@karaokexmas@gmail.com`, which has two @ signs and is not a valid address. Fix before sending — see [[venue-details|Venue Details]].
- Consider adding an overtime rate. As written, there is no charge if the crew runs past 2pm, and film shoots routinely do.
