# NDA Practical Guide

**Summary:** The practical companion to the NDA template — short spoken and written statements to use before showing anyone your work, plus a brief to hand to an attorney and a list of documents still needed if someone becomes an actual cofounder. The source is explicit that this part stays **outside** the signed agreement.

## Key Points
- **Do not put any of this inside the signed legal agreement.** Use it separately.
- Two ready-made statements: one to say before a demo, one to text before sending a link.
- The attorney brief names the state (**New York**) and the eleven specific clauses to review.
- An NDA protects secrecy only — it does **not** settle equity, ownership, vesting, payment, title, or firing rights.

## Pre-Demo Statement
Say this before showing anything:

> Before I show you anything — everything you see, hear, access, test, discuss, or receive from me about my apps, websites, software, systems, workflows, code, designs, prompts, business plans, features, products, and future ideas is confidential and belongs to me. You cannot copy it, use it, disclose it, recreate it, upload it to any tool, show it to anyone, build from it, or make anything similar from it without my written permission. This applies to anything I already made, anything I am making now, and anything I later develop from what I show or explain to you.

## Text Message Before Sending a Link
```
Before I send this, you agree that anything I show, send, explain, demo, or give
access to involving my apps, websites, software, workflows, designs, code,
prompts, business ideas, or future plans is confidential and belongs to me. You
may not copy, use, share, upload, recreate, reverse engineer, build from, or
disclose it to anyone without my written permission.
```

## Attorney Review Brief
Hand this to a New York attorney:

**Client goal:** A one-way NDA, non-use, non-circumvention and IP assignment agreement protecting current and future apps, websites, SaaS products, AI workflows, source code, designs, business models, and service-business / hospitality software concepts.

**Relationship type:** Friend / potential cofounder / possible contractor. **No equity should be implied** unless separately documented.

**State:** New York.

**Key legal concerns to review:**
1. Consideration for current and prior disclosures
2. Retroactive protection for information already disclosed
3. Scope of "future apps" and "planned products"
4. Non-use language, to avoid it being treated as a de facto non-compete
5. Work Product assignment language for cofounder/contractor scenarios
6. Treatment of pre-existing code, templates, libraries, and AI-generated output
7. AI-tool confidentiality restrictions
8. GitHub / Vercel / Supabase / Figma / Wix / Replit account ownership and access control
9. Non-circumvention period and scope
10. Attorney's fees clause
11. Whether arbitration, jury waiver, or emergency injunctive relief language should be added

Also ask whether a separate cofounder agreement, contractor agreement, equity agreement, or IP assignment confirmation is needed.

## If They Become a Real Cofounder
The NDA is not enough on its own. Documents still needed:
- Cofounder agreement
- IP assignment confirmation
- Equity vesting agreement
- Operating agreement or shareholder agreement
- Confidentiality and invention assignment agreement
- Account-access checklist
- GitHub / Vercel / Supabase / Figma / domain ownership checklist

## The Line That Matters Most
> The NDA protects secrecy. It does not by itself solve equity, founder ownership, voting rights, vesting, payment, title, firing rights, or who owns the company.
