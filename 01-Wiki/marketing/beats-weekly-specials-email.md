# Beats Karaoke — Weekly Specials Email

**Summary:** A win-back email to past Beats Karaoke customers listing a different special for every night Sunday through Thursday, plus a "secret special" only people who received the email can claim by mentioning it. It is aimed squarely at the slow weeknights and gives a reason to come in on each one.

## Key Points
- Covers **Sunday through Thursday** — exactly the slow nights.
- **Monday is the strongest offer**: $6 per person per hour for room rentals.
- **Thursday** is $5 draft beer plus a free shot, private room only.
- The "secret special" requires mentioning the email — which makes the campaign **trackable**.
- Sent from beatskaraoke.com / 718-302-3030.

## The Weekly Specials
| Night | Special |
|---|---|
| **Sunday** | $6 Cocktails |
| **Monday** | $6 per person per hour for karaoke room rentals |

| **Thursday** | $5 Draft Beer + Free Shot (private room only) |

## Why the Secret Special Is the Smart Part
Because customers have to mention the email to claim it, every redemption is a measurable result from the campaign. That is exactly the tracking gap called out in [[manager-argument-the-numbers|Manager Argument — The Numbers]] — knowing which marketing actually produced customers instead of guessing.
