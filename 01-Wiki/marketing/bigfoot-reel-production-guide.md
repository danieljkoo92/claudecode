# Bigfoot Reel — Production Guide

**Summary:** A complete how-to for actually making the Bigfoot and Yeti reel, using CapCut and Pika Labs to composite animated characters over real footage of the store. It covers filming, generating the character clips, compositing, sound design, Instagram optimization, and a second-by-second on-screen text flow. The punchline lands on BYOB, which makes the whole thing an advert disguised as a joke.

## Key Points
- **CapCut + Pika Labs** is the recommended route — near-free and Instagram-native.
- Runway is the paid alternative: better lighting match and automatic motion tracking, but costs credits and renders slower.
- Film **vertical (9:16), 8–12 seconds, slow or static camera** — that footage is the background layer.
- Keep the finished reel **10–20 seconds**. Short means more replays.
- **The first 2–3 seconds must hook** — people watch without sound, so text does the work.

## Why This Workflow
- Vertical format
- Clean compositing — characters over real store video
- Looks intentionally comedic rather than uncanny
- No large costs

## Step 1 — Film the Store Video
- Vertical (9:16)
- 8–12 seconds
- Slow or static camera
- Keep lighting consistent

This becomes the background plate.

## Step 2 — Generate the Character Clips
Use Pika Labs (free credits for new users). Prompt:

```
Cartoonish drunken Bigfoot + white Sasquatch sneaking beers, comedic exaggerated
movements, neon lighting, playful vibe, loopable animated clip, transparent
background if possible.
```

Export a 5–10 second loop, preferably with an alpha (transparent) channel if Pika will give one.

## Step 3 — Composite in CapCut
1. Import the store video, set as base layer
2. Import the Bigfoot/Sasquatch animations, place on top
3. Use cutout / masking / layering
4. Scale the characters appropriately
5. Slide them walking into frame
6. Add motion tracking if they need to follow a lateral path
7. Add sound and text captions — punchlines appear fast, at Instagram tempo

## Sound Design
- Clumsy footsteps
- Slurred comical voices
- Record scratch when the employee appears
- Boing / slide whistle cues
- Text overlay dialogue bubbles

## Instagram Optimization
- **First 2–3 seconds must hook** — e.g. *"Wait… WHAT are these guys doing? 😭"*
- Bold captions and emojis — people watch without sound first
- Keep it 10–20 seconds
- Trending audio if it fits, otherwise punchy SFX
- On-screen text fast and funny, **bold white outline** so it reads instantly

## The On-Screen Text Flow
| Time | Text |
|---|---|
| 0–2s | "When Bigfoot & Sasquatch sneak BEER into karaoke…" |
| 3–5s | Whisper-yelling: "Act natural!" |
| 6–8s | Employee pops in 😳 |
| 9–11s | Employee: "Chill… BYOB is allowed." |
| 12–15s | Bigfoot: "You could've said that!" |
| 15–18s | They're already singing off-key 🎤😂 |

## Why the Ending Is the Whole Point
The joke resolves on **"BYOB is allowed."** That is the single most important fact the venue wants people to know — it is the centre of the pricing strategy and the corporate pitch — and here it arrives as a punchline instead of an advert. That is what makes this worth filming over any other reel idea on the list.
