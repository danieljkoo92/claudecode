# Bigfoot and Yeti Beer Sneak — Video Prompt

**Summary:** A finished AI video generation prompt for a 5-second clip set at the karaoke bar entrance. A brown humanoid Bigfoot and a tall white Yeti sneak beer into the hallway using exaggerated cartoon stealth — deep crouches, tiptoeing, heads darting around — while the lighting stays realistic to match the uploaded entrance photo. The reference given is Tom from Tom and Jerry creeping past a sleeping guard dog.

## Key Points
- Background: the uploaded **entrance image** of the venue.
- Two characters, visible the entire time: **brown humanoid Bigfoot** and **tall white Yeti**.
- They are sneaking beer cases into the hallway, clutching them like contraband.
- Movement is cartoonishly exaggerated but the render stays **realistic cinematic**.
- Length: **5 seconds**. Handheld camera with a slight nervous micro-shake.
- Entry through the glass doors on the **right**, exiting frame to the **left**.

## The Prompt
```
Using the uploaded entrance image as the background.

Add TWO characters that remain visible the entire time:
  Brown humanoid Bigfoot
  Tall white Yeti

They are attempting to secretly sneak beer into the hallway.

Both characters move in an exaggerated stealth manner:
• Deep crouch posture (hips low, knees bent 90 degrees)
• Walking extremely slowly on the tips of their toes
• Shoulders hunched forward
• Elbows tucked in tightly
• Heads darting left and right every second
• Quick paranoid eye movements
• Brief freezing mid-step as if they heard something
• Bigfoot presses flat against the wall and slides sideways
• Yeti tiptoes carefully and lifts each foot very slowly before placing it
  down silently

They clutch the beer cases tightly to their chests like they are hiding
contraband. Overly dramatic spy-style stealth behavior.

Add subtle tense sneaking music energy in body language (no audio required,
just mood). Handheld camera with slight nervous micro-shake. Realistic
lighting that matches the entrance image.

5 seconds.

"Behavior exaggerated like a cartoon burglar sneaking past security cameras,
but rendered in realistic cinematic style."
```

## Camera Direction
Have them come into frame through the glass doors on the right side, walking toward the left and out of frame. The look should be exaggerated and cartoonishly sneaky — like Tom from Tom and Jerry walking past the dog or a sleeping grandma.
