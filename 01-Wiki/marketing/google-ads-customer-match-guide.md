# Google Ads Customer Match Guide

**Summary:** A step-by-step guide to getting Google to find new customers who resemble existing ones, using the Customer Match feature. It covers building the email list, uploading it to Google Ads, how Google builds the lookalike audience, which locations to target for this venue, and why installing a conversion tag makes the whole thing get smarter over time.

## Key Points
- The feature is called **Customer Match** — Google needs your existing customer list first.
- Minimum useful list: **100 emails**. Good: **500+**. Great: **1,000+**.
- Location targeting for this venue: **Queens, Flushing, Bayside, Nassau County, NYC**, radius **10–20 miles**.
- A **conversion tag** on the website is what teaches Google who actually books.
- Google can build audiences of birthday planners and people searching karaoke — the most profitable customers.

## 1. Build a Customer Email List
Create a spreadsheet with:
- Email
- Phone (optional but helpful)
- First name (optional)
- Last name (optional)

Aim for **at least 100–300 contacts** for good results. Save it as a CSV.

## 2. Upload the List to Google Ads
At ads.google.com:
1. Tools & Settings
2. Shared Library
3. Audience Manager
4. Segments
5. Click **+**
6. Select **Customer List**
7. Upload the CSV

Google matches those emails against Google users.

## 3. Google Builds Similar Audiences
Once uploaded, Google analyzes search behaviour, locations, interests, nightlife activity and group events, then finds people who behave like your customers — for example people who search "birthday party near me" or "karaoke queens", visit nightlife websites, or attend group events.

## 4. Use That Audience in Ads
Under audience targeting, the audiences Google may build include:
- Similar to your past customers
- People searching karaoke
- Birthday planners
- Local nightlife groups

## 5. Add Location Targeting
Critical for a venue. Target:
- Queens
- Flushing
- Bayside
- Nassau County
- NYC

Distance radius: **10–20 miles**. This prevents wasted ad spend.

## 6. Use Conversion Signals
Install a conversion tag on the website. When someone books, Google learns what kind of person converts, and over time finds more of them.

## What Good Looks Like
Instead of ads reaching random people, Google targets people planning birthdays, groups searching nightlife, people who visited karaoke websites, and people similar to previous customers.
