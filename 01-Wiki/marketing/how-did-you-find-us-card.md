# "How Did You Find Us?" Card

**Summary:** A five-option tick-box card given to customers to record how they discovered the venue. It is short enough to fill in at the desk, and it is the simplest possible fix for the biggest measurement gap in the business — nobody knowing which marketing actually brings people in.

## Key Points
- Five options only — fast enough that people actually complete it.
- Separates **Google search** from **Google Maps**, which are different channels with different fixes.
- Captures **Friend/Referral**, which is the channel comps and free drinks are supposed to generate.
- Captures **Returning customer**, which separates retention from acquisition.

## The Card
```
How did you find us?
Please ✔️

[  ] Instagram
[  ] Google search
[  ] Google maps
[  ] Friend/Referral
[  ] Returning customer
```

## Why This Small Card Matters More Than It Looks
The most repeated complaint in these notes is that nobody tracks where customers come from — see [[manager-argument-the-numbers|Manager Argument — The Numbers]], which lists "you don't calculate where the customers are coming from" as a core failure, and notes $1,700 of ad spend with the results never checked.

This card answers that for the price of printing. A month of these gives you:
- Whether Instagram ads produce anyone (the claim in the notes is zero)
- Whether Google Maps or Google search is doing the work — which decides whether to invest in [[google-ads-customer-match-guide|ads]] or Maps ranking
- A real referral rate, which is the number that decides whether comping drinks pays for itself
- A returning-customer rate, which is the number that decides everything else

## Making It Work
- Hand it over with the ID and card at check-in, when people are already standing still — see the greeting SOP in [[complete-sop-system|Complete SOP System]].
- Tally the results weekly onto the same sheet as sales, so channel and revenue sit side by side.
- Keep the option list frozen. Changing the options mid-way makes months uncomparable.
