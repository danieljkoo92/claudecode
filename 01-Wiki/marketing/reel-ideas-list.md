# Reel Ideas List

**Summary:** A short list of specific reel concepts for the karaoke bar, written as shorthand. They range from a waiter POV bit and a big-order gag to themed room reveals and BYOB content.

## Key Points
- Six ideas, all quick shorthand rather than scripts.
- Two are themed-room reveals — galaxy and K-pop — which tie into the birthday themed-room offer.
- Two are BYOB-related, matching the BYOB strategy.
- The Bigfoot/Yeti characters reappear here, this time tied to BYOB.
