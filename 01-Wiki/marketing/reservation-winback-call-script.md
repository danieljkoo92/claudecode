# Reservation Win-Back Call Script

**Summary:** A phone approach for calling old reservations and leads with a deal. The distinctive part is the referral mechanic: if the customer can't use the offer themselves, they can hand it to a friend or family member, and doing so earns them a **guaranteed** deal next time rather than a random one.

## Key Points
- Call **marked old reservations and leads** with a specific deal and script.
- Quote them **an estimated saving based on their previous party size** — a real number, not a vague discount.
- They can **pass the deal to a friend or family member** to use in their place.
- If they pass it on, it gets tracked and they get **another deal next time, guaranteed instead of random**.

## The Approach
1. Work from the list of marked old reservations and leads.
2. Give them the deal using the script.
3. **Estimate what they would save**, based on the size of their previous reservation.
4. Tell them they can give the deal to friends or family to use in their place.
5. If they do, we track it — and they get another deal next time, **guaranteed, instead of random**.

## Why the Referral Mechanic Is Clever
Most win-back calls fail on the same objection: the person isn't planning a night out right now. This script removes that. The offer stops being "come back this month" and becomes "you have something worth giving away" — which costs the customer nothing and still fills a room.

The guarantee is the second half. A vague "we'll look after you next time" is worth nothing; **"guaranteed instead of random"** is a specific promise that makes passing the deal on feel like a trade rather than a favour.

This is also the single highest-leverage play identified in [[marketing-economics-argument|Marketing Economics]] — *"the most effective thing available right now is getting someone to recommend us to a friend for that friend's birthday."* This script is how that actually gets done.

## Needs Before Using
- **A way to track who passed a deal to whom.** The whole mechanic depends on it, and it does not work on memory.
- **The savings figures per party size**, worked out in advance so nobody is doing arithmetic on the phone. The tiers in [[package-tiers-and-design|Package Tiers]] already give per-person savings of $11 to $30 — multiply by party size and the number is ready.
