# School Field Trip Quote Email

**Summary:** A quote sent to a teacher organizing an honor roll field trip for about 50 students and 5 chaperones. It reserves both VIP rooms exclusively, and steers the booking to before 5pm to catch the happy hour room rate — $300 per hour for both rooms instead of $240 per room. It also spells out the exact tax, gratuity and card fee percentages.

## Key Points
- Group: **~50 students plus 5 chaperones** — both VIP rooms reserved exclusively.
- Standard evening rate: **$240 per hour per VIP room**.
- **Happy hour rate: $300 per hour for both rooms** — booked before 5pm.
- With **8.875% tax** and **19% gratuity**: $383.63 per hour.
- Paying by card adds a **3.5% processing fee**: $394.13 per hour.

## The Pricing Structure
This email is the clearest statement of how the venue's charges stack:

| Layer | Rate |
|---|---|
| Sales tax | 8.875% |
| Gratuity | 19% |
| Card processing fee | 3.5% |

Worked through on the $300/hour happy hour rate:
- $300 base
- **$383.63** including tax and gratuity
- **$394.13** if paid by card

## The Email
```
Good afternoon Ms. Basilio,

Thank you for reaching out and for thinking of us for your honor roll field
trip. We would be happy to accommodate your group of approximately 50 students
and 5 chaperones.

For a group that size, we would reserve both of our VIP private rooms
exclusively for your school. Our standard evening rate is $240 per hour per VIP
room (plus tax and gratuity). We do recommend an earlier field-trip time,
starting the room before 5:00pm to get 2 hours at the happy hour room rate. We
can offer you the happy hour room rate of $300 per hour total for both VIP
rooms.

At that rate, the pricing works out to approximately:

  $300 per hour for both VIP rooms
  $383.63 per hour including 8.875% tax and 19% gratuity
  $394.13 per hour if paid by credit/debit card, which includes a 3.5% card
  processing fee

Please let us know your preferred date in early February, the start time, and
how many hours you would like to reserve, and we can draft a detailed estimate
and hold the rooms for your group.

Thank you again, and we look forward to the possibility of hosting your
students.

Best regards,
Beats Karaoke Team
```

## Why the Happy Hour Steer Is Smart
Two VIP rooms at the standard rate would be $480/hour. Offering $300 for both looks like a large discount, but it fills two rooms in the dead pre-5pm window that would otherwise earn nothing — and a school group is exactly the kind of booking that can only happen early anyway.
