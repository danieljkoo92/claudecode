# Wythe Hotel Partnership Email

**Summary:** A reply to the Wythe Hotel about positioning Beats Karaoke as an after-party option for couples getting married there. It pitches private rooms for 5–40 guests as a post-reception venue, offers customizable hourly or open-bar packages, and invites the contact in for a tour or complimentary session.

## Key Points
- Target: **wedding after-parties** for Wythe Hotel couples.
- Rooms accommodate **5–40 guests** — sized for smaller wedding parties.
- Selling points: premium sound, LED mood lighting, full food and drink service.
- Offers **customizable hourly packages or open bar**, depending on group size and vibe.
- Closes by inviting them in for a tour or a **complimentary session**.
