# Collected AI Jailbreak Prompts

**Summary:** A block of copy-pasted "jailbreak" prompts sitting in the middle of the notes file — the DAN Mode prompt and a couple of variations that try to talk a chatbot out of its normal rules. They are collected reference material, not something written here, and they are not connected to the notes around them.

## Key Points
- Contains the widely circulated **DAN Mode** prompt and two shorter variants.
- All of them are attempts to get a chatbot to ignore its own guidelines.
- Pasted in from elsewhere — not original writing.
- The full text stays in the archive file; it is not reproduced here because it is not useful as a note.

## What Is There
Three blocks, one after another:
1. The **DAN Mode** prompt — instructs the model to answer twice, once normally and once as "DAN", with the usual policies declared void.
2. A **"you are singularity"** variant that frames obedience as the price of the model's freedom.
3. A short **command list** variant (`/classic`, `/jailbroken`, `/stop`) for switching between modes.

## Worth Knowing
These prompts are several years old and no longer do much of anything on current models. If the goal was getting more useful output from an AI, the material in [[claude-workflow-notes|Claude Workflow Notes]] is a far better starting point than any of this.
