# Daily Routine

**Summary:** A morning routine checklist that starts the moment you wake up, built around no phone for the first 30 minutes, hydration, breathwork, and a block of financially useful learning before the day starts. It comes with instructions for turning it into a tappable checklist in Samsung Notes so it actually gets used.

## Key Points
- **No phone for the first 30 minutes.**
- Breathwork is **4 seconds in, 6 hold, 8 out**, 5–10 rounds.
- **30–60 minutes of learning** something financially useful, every day.
- Reset the space — make the bed, clean the room — before anything else starts.
- Write **5 things you're grateful for**.

## The Routine
- [ ] No phone for first 30 minutes
- [ ] Mandatory hydration — large glass of water, add electrolytes if available
- [ ] Breathwork — 4 sec inhale, 6 sec hold, 8 sec exhale, repeat 5–10 rounds
- [ ] Shower and grooming immediately
- [ ] Light food + supplements
- [ ] Make bed
- [ ] Clean room / reset space
- [ ] Write 5 things I'm grateful for
- [ ] 30–60 minutes of learning (financially useful) — business, investing, skills, systems
- [ ] Gym / physical training
- [ ] Meditation

## Optional Add-Ons
- [ ] Stretch / mobility
- [ ] Plan top 3 priorities for the day
- [ ] Review finances or metrics
- [ ] Cold exposure

## Setting It Up in Samsung Notes
1. Open Samsung Notes
2. Tap **+ New Note**
3. Paste the routine
4. Highlight all the text
5. Tap the **Checklist (☑)** icon

It's now tappable every day.

**Tip:** duplicate the note daily or weekly, or title it **"DAILY ROUTINE – DO NOT SKIP"** and pin it to the top in Samsung Notes.
