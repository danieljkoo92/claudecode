# Dream Journal — Korean Cab

**Summary:** A dream written down immediately on waking at 4:57am on 05/24/25. In it, he is in the back of a Korean cab with his mother. She takes napkins, the driver complains about sneezing and dust and takes them back. His mother says she loves him and that she's too tired, she's going to sleep — and he tells her to go home and sleep, then wakes up.

## The Dream
Back of a Korean cab. Mom took napkins. The cab driver complained about sneezing and dust, and took the napkins back.

> 사랑해. 나 너무 피곤해서 잘래.
> *(I love you. I'm so tired, I'm going to sleep.)*

He says:
> 집에 가서 자야지
> *(You should go home and sleep.)*

And wakes up.
