# Cold Call Script — Zero Risk Website Offer

**Summary:** A phone script for cold calling businesses that have no website, built around a revenue-share offer instead of an upfront fee. The pitch is that the business pays nothing until results are proven, then either pays a cut of sales generated or buys the site outright — which is presented as the cheaper option long term.

## Key Points
- Lead with **no risk and no cost until results are proven**.
- Two options offered: **revenue share** on sales generated, or **buy the website** outright.
- Buying is framed as the option that saves money in the end.
- They can run it free first and switch plans once results come in.
- A "customer generated" is defined as someone who orders fully through the app.
- Requires **transparency from the business** so the count is not fudged.
- Free extra services are thrown in if they do buy the website.

## The Script

**Opening:**
> "Hi, I was wondering if a manager or owner was available to speak to by any chance?"

**When they ask why you're calling** — and they will:
> "I do online marketing, but I formatted a system where you guys have no risk or cost until the results are proven. I just saw that you guys don't have a website or any online presence, so I want to see if I can improve that with zero risk to you of course. I created an option to buy online products — don't worry about anything up front — and then I'll ask for a piece of the sales that I generate for you."

**The alternative option:**
> "And of course there are other options where you actually buy the website, which will probably save you money at the end of the day. But for the extra customers that I bring, with zero risk, it will essentially be free and no cost to you."

**Lowering the commitment further:**
> "You guys can try it. You can run it for free for now, and once the results start coming in you can buy the website and change the plan later as well."

**Setting the terms:**
> "What I consider a customer that I generate is someone who orders fully through the app. I'll also require some transparency on your end so I don't get broken off. And we supply free services when you do plan on buying the website."
