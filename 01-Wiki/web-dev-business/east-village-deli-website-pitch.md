# East Village Deli — Website Pitch

**Summary:** A written revenue projection prepared for the owner of an East Village deli in Manhattan to justify building a website. It argues four sources of gain — recovering delivery app commissions, capturing catering orders, picking up local search traffic, and stopping the credibility loss of having no website after ten years in business. Every figure is deliberately cut to half of standard industry estimates so the projection under-promises.

## Key Points
- All numbers were **halved from standard industry estimates** on purpose, to stay conservative.
- Delivery apps take **25–30% commission** per order; direct ordering wins some of that back.
- Conservative case adds **$760/month, $9,150/year**; strong case **$3,200/month, $38,400/year**.
- Even at half the conservative case, about **$4,500/year** is still attainable.

## 1. Recovering Third-Party Delivery App Fees
DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub charge roughly 25–30% per order. A direct ordering system on the deli's own site shifts part of the existing customer base to ordering direct, recovering commission that is currently lost.
- Monthly: **$250 – $300**
- Annual: **$3,000 – $3,600**

## 2. Catering and Bulk Orders
The East Village has offices, NYU-affiliated facilities, production studios and event venues. Without a catering page the business cannot capture that demand at all.
- Monthly: **$400 – $2,400**
- Annual: **$4,800 – $28,800**

## 3. New Customers Through Local Search
A Google Business listing alone gives limited visibility for high-intent searches like "best deli East Village" or "breakfast Avenue A". A site with local SEO captures those and converts them into walk-ins and pickup orders.
- Monthly: **$110 – $500**
- Annual: **$1,350 – $6,000**

## 4. Preventing Credibility Loss
For a business open more than ten years, having no website can suggest to prospective customers that it has closed or is unreliable. This hits hardest with younger customers who check online before visiting.
- Annual loss prevention: **~$2,700**

## Projected Impact
| Scenario | Additional Monthly | Additional Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | $760 | $9,150 |
| Realistic | $1,700 | $20,400 |
| Strong | $3,200 | $38,400 |

Even if every projection above is overstated and actual results reach only half of the conservative scenario, roughly **$4,500 or more** in additional annual revenue is still attainable. The site pays back its cost quickly and keeps returning for years after.
