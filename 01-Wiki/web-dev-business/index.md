## Notes
- [[east-village-deli-website-pitch|East Village Deli — Website Pitch]] — a written revenue projection, all figures deliberately halved
- [[auto-shop-cold-call-script|Auto Shop Cold Call Script]] — built on the brakes story: "you lost me as a customer for $500"

## The Pattern Across All Three
Each one leads with the customer's lost money rather than the service being sold, and each ends with something free — a concept, a trial, a preview. Nobody is asked to buy anything in the first conversation.
