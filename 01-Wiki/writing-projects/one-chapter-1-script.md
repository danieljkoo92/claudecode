# One — Chapter 1 Script

**Summary:** A professional page-by-page manga script for the opening chapter, in which ten-year-old One protects his little sister from four older bullies outside a Flushing elementary school. Eight pages, right-to-left, panel by panel with camera direction, dialogue, sound effects and expression notes. Two rules govern the whole chapter: One never speaks, and he is not properly introduced until after the fight is over.

## Key Points
- **8 pages**, right-to-left reading order, 5–6 panels each.
- Tone: **grounded, cinematic, Asian-American crime drama** — not stylized action.
- **Visual rule: One never speaks.**
- **Story rule: One is not properly introduced until after the fight ends.**
- Setting: sunny weekday afternoon, after school, **Flushing, Queens**.
- Fight style: fast, flashy, precise — kicks, elbows, knees, slips, pivots, counters.

## Page 1 — The Bullying
Four older kids (12–13) corner his 7-year-old sister against a chain-link fence. Her backpack is held away from her, her lunch container kicked along the ground, papers and pencils scattered.

- **Panel 2** — "Pick it up." *(mocking grin)*
- **Panel 3** — Low shot near the ground. She reaches for the bag; another bully nudges the lunch container away with his shoe. **SFX: CLACK**
- **Panel 4** — A bully shoves her into the fence. **SFX: THUD**
- **Panel 5** — Close-up. Her eyes water but she refuses to cry. Off-panel: *"Go back where you came from."*
- **Panel 6** — The ringleader sneers into her face: *"You hear me, C—?"* At the very edge of frame, **a child-sized sneaker steps into panel.**

## Page 2 — One Enters
**Purpose:** One appears and attacks instantly. No warning, no dialogue.
**Page-turn hook:** the fight has begun before the reader properly sees him.

- **Panel 1** — Extreme close-up, ground level: the sister's fallen keychain charm on the pavement. A sneaker stops beside it.
- **Panel 2** — A small hand picks up the keychain.
- **Panel 3** — Before the bullies notice him, One drives a sharp front kick into the ringleader's midsection. **SFX: THUMP**
- **Panel 4** — The ringleader slams into the chain-link fence. **SFX: RATTLE**

## Page 7 — The Walk Home
**Purpose:** establish Flushing and the sibling relationship.
**Page-turn hook:** someone is watching them from a distance.

- **Panel 1** — Cinematic wide: the two walking through Flushing in late-afternoon light. Storefronts, apartment buildings, people heading home, multilingual neighbourhood.
  > *Narration: He never explained what he did.*
- **Panel 2** — One carries her backpack while she holds his hand.
  > *Narration: He never made a speech.*
- **Panel 3** — Close-up on their hands. Her small hand grips his tightly.
- **Panel 4** — She leans slightly toward him while walking. He keeps looking straight ahead.
  > *Narration: He just took her home.*
- **Panel 5** — Distant observational shot — someone watching.

## Why the Structure Works
Three narration boxes on page 7 carry the entire characterization — *he never explained, he never made a speech, he just took her home* — and they do it after a wordless fight. That is the same character described in the canon: someone whose actions are the argument. Holding the introduction until after the violence means the reader meets him the way the other kids do.
